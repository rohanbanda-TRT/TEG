# Venture Capital & Investors — TEG Ecosystem

> **Purpose:** Consolidated record of the VC / investor side of the Tech Expo Gujarat ecosystem — distinct from paid **sponsors** (see `sponsors_partners/sponsors_and_partners.md`) and from **exhibitors** (see `exhibitors/exhibitors_directory.md`).
> **Last updated:** August 31, 2026
> **Key distinction:** VCs are **not a sponsorship tier**. They participate through TEG's **investor–founder matchmaking track**, which to date has been run mainly at the **TEG Business Retreat** (Dec 2025) and **TEG Ignite** (Apr 2026), not on the main expo floor. For TEG 2026 expo, investor-facing opportunity is implied by the organizers but no named 2026 VC list is published yet.

---

## 1. Where VCs fit in the TEG ecosystem

| Track | Event | VC involvement |
|---|---|---|
| **Main expo floor** | TEG 2026 (27–29 Nov 2026) | No named VC list published. Organizers position the expo as investor-adjacent ("Premium VC & Startup Access" is a sponsor-brochure pitch line), and the sponsorship deck lists a **VC network / ₹1.5 crore funding track record** as an exhibitor benefit — but no confirmed 2026 investor roster. |
| **Boardroom / retreat** | TEG Business Retreat 2025 (19–20 Dec 2025, Anand) | **15+ VCs**, combined portfolio value cited as **₹3,000 Cr**. Dedicated "Submit Pitch Deck" channel + "Founder Pitch Pass" (₹7,500) giving founders a direct pitch slot. Headline result: **₹1.5 crore raised in one day**. |
| **Curtain-raiser** | TEG Ignite 2026 (3 Apr 2026) | Investor-heavy speaker lineup and two "Founder's Pitch (VC round)" parallel tracks; panels on raising vs. selling and "Funding Beyond Equity". |

*The Retreat and Ignite feed directly into the TEG 2026 program — see `related_events/related_events.md` for full agendas.*
