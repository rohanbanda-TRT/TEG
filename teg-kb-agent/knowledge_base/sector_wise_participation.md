## Overview

This document tracks which companies participate in each sector and how sector representation evolves from TEG 2024 to TEG 2026.

**Data Quality Note:** All companies listed below are verified from official sources (techexpogujarat.com/exhibitors or company websites). The Emgage conflict is noted separately.

**Multi-Domain Companies:** Many companies operate across multiple technology domains (e.g., AI + Cloud, Software Dev + E-commerce, etc.). These companies are listed in each relevant sector with cross-references noted in the "Notes" column. The summary table reflects total sector participations, not unique company counts.

## Sector-Specific Value Propositions

### For AI & Machine Learning Companies
TEG is positioned as "Gujarat's Beacon of Rising Innovation & AI." AI companies get:
- Direct access to 15,000+ decision-makers seeking AI solutions
- The "Experience Zone" for live AI demonstrations
- Networking with VCs interested in AI startups
- Association with Gujarat's AI transformation narrative

### For Software Development & IT Services
The largest sector at TEG 2024 with 22 companies. Benefits include:
- Access to 15,000+ business visitors from manufacturing, retail, healthcare, finance
- Pre-scheduled B2B meetings
- Multi-industry exposure
- Live product demonstration opportunities

### For Fintech Companies
TEG connects fintech companies with banking, finance, and insurance sector decision-makers. Benefits include:
- Access to finance industry leaders
- Networking with investors
- Association with Gujarat's growing fintech ecosystem

### For Cloud & Infrastructure Companies
Cloud providers benefit from:
- Direct access to IT decision-makers
- Demonstration of cloud solutions to 15,000+ visitors
- Networking with enterprise buyers
