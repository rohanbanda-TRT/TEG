## Overview
Glasier Inc is an Ahmedabad-based company, founded in 2017, offering IT staff-augmentation services (developers, AI specialists, designers) alongside custom software development, web/hybrid app design, and digital marketing services.

## Company Details
- Founded 2017.
- Located at A/6, First Floor, Safal Profitaire, Corporate Rd, opp. Hotel Ramada, Prahlad Nagar, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India 380015.
- 34 employees.
- Operates across multiple countries including India, US, UK, Canada, Singapore, and UAE.
