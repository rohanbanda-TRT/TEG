## Overview
Capermint Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a mobile game and app development company headquartered in Ahmedabad, with an associate office in Quebec, Canada, serving clients globally. Since 2014 it has provided mobile game, website, and mobile-application development, including competency in Augmented Reality, Virtual Reality, and Mixed Reality app development, plus a dedicated Ahmedabad testing lab for project QA.

## Company Details
- Founded 2014.
- Head office: 304–305, Shivalik Shilp, Iscon Cross Road, Ahmedabad.
- Approximately $3 million in revenue and 122 employees (IT Services / IT Consulting sector).
