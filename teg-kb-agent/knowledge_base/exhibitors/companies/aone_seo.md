## Overview
AONE SEO Service Pvt Ltd is an Ahmedabad-based digital marketing agency established in 2013, offering fully integrated services: SEO, SMO, ORM, PPC, ASO, content marketing, branding, UI/UX design, e-commerce SEO, e-commerce store development, and web development. Its founder, **Pruthviraj Zala**, is a co-organizer of Tech Expo Gujarat, and AONE SEO is also the agency that built and promotes the official TEG website — a dual organizer-plus-vendor relationship.
