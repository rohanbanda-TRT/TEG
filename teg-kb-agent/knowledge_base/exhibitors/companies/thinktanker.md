## Overview
ThinkTanker is a web, mobile, and e-commerce development and enterprise-technology consulting company founded in 2014, engineering AI, SaaS, ERP, commerce, and enterprise software systems for global businesses. Its founder-CEO **Rajan Rawal** is one of the twelve core organizers of Tech Expo Gujarat. The company also builds the Connecti5 contact-management product (see `connecti5.md`).

## Company Details
- **Founded:** 2014.
- **Founder(s) / leadership:** Rajan Rawal — Founder & CEO (B.E. Computer Science, Sarvajanik College of Engineering & Technology; also TEG core organizer); Nency Rawal — Co-Founder & Business Development Manager.
- **Team size:** ~60+ employees; clients across 11+ countries.
- **HQ:** Ahmedabad (Vastral), Gujarat.
- **Tech stack / focus:** PHP, Node.js, Angular, Laravel, React/React Native, Shopify; AI-driven e-commerce personalization, ERP, custom enterprise software.
- **Contact:** via thinktanker.com.

> **Naming note:** The brochure entry was described as "ThinkTanker Technosys, Surat." Public records and this KB's organizer file consistently place ThinkTanker (Rajan Rawal's company) in **Ahmedabad** and reference the entity name "THINKTANKER Technosoft Pvt Ltd" — the "Technosys/Surat" descriptor is treated as an imprecise brochure label, not a separate company.
