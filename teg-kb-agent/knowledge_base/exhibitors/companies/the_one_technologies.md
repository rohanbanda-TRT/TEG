# The One Technologies

> **Category:** Custom Software Development / Mobile, Web, Cloud, IT Consulting

## Overview

The One Technologies is an Ahmedabad-founded (2010) offshore web and software development company, now with additional offices in the US, Canada, and Germany. It offers mobile app development, website development, open-source customization, custom software development, and graphics design, with roughly 150 IT professionals and 500+ completed projects.

## Company Details

- **Founded:** 2010, Ahmedabad
- **Team size:** ~150 IT professionals (developers, designers, QA engineers, digital marketing experts, business development managers)
- **Track record:** 500+ successful projects
- **Offices:** Ahmedabad (HQ), US, Canada, Germany
- **Services:** Mobile app development, website development, custom software development, open-source customization, SEO, e-commerce solutions, internet marketing, online branding, enterprise solutions, graphics design

## TEG Participation

Confirmed TEG 2024 exhibitor at **Booth E2**, December 20–21, 2024, Vigyan Bhavan, Science City, Ahmedabad — confirmed via the company's own blog post announcing its participation. Exhibition theme: "Empowering Businesses Through Technology." Showcased custom software development, mobile/web app development, cloud solutions (Azure, AWS, Google Cloud), IT consulting, CRM/ERP, and UI/UX design, with live demonstrations of interactive dashboards, mobile apps, and cloud integrations. Co-founders and business team members were present at the booth.

**Founder (per podcast) — resolves prior ambiguity:** A dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode, "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 24: Divyesh Gohil, The One" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba), names **Divyesh Gohil** as the guest representing "The One" at TEG 2024. This directly confirms the previously-flagged-as-unconfirmed link noted below: TEG co-organizer Divyesh Gohil is indeed associated with this exhibitor, "The One Technologies."
