## Overview
Perigeon Software Private Limited (formerly Intforce Software) is an Ahmedabad-based global technology consulting and product engineering company specializing in Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365, and enterprise application development on .NET and Power Platform. It is a certified Salesforce Ridge (Silver) Partner and Conga Implementation Partner, serving 15+ industries from offices in India and the USA.
