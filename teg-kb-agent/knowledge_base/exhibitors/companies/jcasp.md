## Overview
Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "JCASP Technologies" sponsor logo directly to **jcasptechnologies.com**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. JCasp Technologies is a web and mobile app development company based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, offering website development, e-commerce development, mobile applications, SEO/SMO, and — per its LinkedIn profile — AI/ML and chatbot/GPT-plugin development. It positions itself as serving clients globally. Note: company registry filings show a "Jcasp Technologies Private Limited" incorporated August 2023, while its LinkedIn/founding narrative elsewhere cites 2010 as a founding year — this discrepancy (a newer Pvt Ltd incorporation vs. an older claimed founding date) was not reconciled in this pass, so treat company age/history with some caution.

**Founder (per podcast):** Independently corroborated by a dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode — "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 46: Jemit Sanghvi" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba) — which names **Jemit Sanghvi** as the "visionary leader of JCasp Technologies," matching this company exactly.

## Company Details
- **Location:** 608 Palladium A Business Hub, opposite 4D Square, Ahmedabad, Gujarat – 380005 (per company registry filing); also operates from Chandkheda, Ahmedabad per business directory listings.
- **Team size:** 11–50 employees (LinkedIn).
- **Specialties:** Responsive web design, e-commerce development, mobile app development, SEO/SMO, AI/ML and chatbot/GPT-plugin development. Technologies cited include PHP, Angular JS, Node.js, Bootstrap, and AWS.
