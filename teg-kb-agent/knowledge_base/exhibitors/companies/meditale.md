## Overview
MediTale ("The MediTale") is a healthcare software platform providing hospital and clinic management — patients, appointments, billing, EMR/electronic patient records, diagnosis and prescription workflows, and centralized secure data storage. It supports multi-facility operation (hospitals, clinics, medical centres) from a central dashboard and positions itself around automation-driven operational efficiency. Tagline: "Smart Cure For Your Care."
