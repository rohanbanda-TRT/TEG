## Overview
TechnoBrains Business Solutions LLP is an Ahmedabad-founded, globally-operating software development company established circa 2008–2009, with offices in Ahmedabad and San Francisco. It is founded and led by **Bhavik Shah**, who is also one of the twelve core organizers of Tech Expo Gujarat — TechnoBrains is directly tied to TEG's own organizing coalition, and has confirmed exhibitor presence at both TEG 2024 and the upcoming TEG 2026.

## Company Details
- **Founded:** c. 2008–2009.
- **Founder & CEO:** Bhavik Shah (Masters from Northumbria University, UK; Bachelor's from KS School of Business Management; 15+ years IT experience).
- **Scale:** 200+ clients worldwide, 350+ projects completed, 75+ team members.
- **Service lines:** AI development (consulting, custom development, integration), web development (ASP.NET, Node.js, Angular, React, PHP, Laravel, WordPress), mobile app development (iOS, Android, Flutter, React Native, Ionic), e-commerce development, enterprise solutions (SaaS, ERP, CRM, MVP, CMS), cloud services (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud), UI/UX design.
- **Industries served:** Healthcare, hospitality, education, HRMS, e-commerce, food delivery, real estate, sports.
