## Overview
Codemech Solutions is an Ahmedabad-based software development company established in 2016, delivering web, mobile, cloud, IoT, and Java-based enterprise software. Services include web and mobile app development, SaaS platforms, DevOps automation, cloud consulting, CMS, and enterprise IT. It reports 60+ projects and 50+ global clients across the USA, UK, Canada, Ireland, Australia, South Africa, and India.
