## Overview
Integrax Technolabs LLP is a young (incorporated December 2024) India-based custom software company focused on AI, AIoT, SaaS, computer vision, and IoT solutions for both startups and enterprises. Its stated mission is to make transformative technologies accessible beyond large enterprises, serving funded startups building first products and established companies modernizing legacy systems.
