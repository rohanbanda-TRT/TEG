## Overview
Apicem Partners is an Ahmedabad-based IT services and consulting firm focused on enterprise infrastructure. Its offerings include managed resources, build-operate-transfer (BOT) engagements, and recruiting services across technical, functional, and value-add domains such as analytics, ERP/CRM customization, and financial management. Tagline: "Architecting the modern enterprise infrastructure."
