## Overview
EaseBiz Fintech Solutions LLP is an Ahmedabad-based consultancy ("Easing the Business") providing accounting and taxation, GST compliance, audit and assurance, income-tax planning, plus placement consultancy and training/development. It positions itself as a one-stop back-office and compliance partner for individuals, corporates, and startups, citing 20+ years of combined experience.
