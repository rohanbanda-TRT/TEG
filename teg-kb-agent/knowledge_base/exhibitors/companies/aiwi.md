## Overview
Aiwi Software Solutions is an Ahmedabad-based SaaS company offering an all-in-one business management platform for small and growing businesses — combining HR, payroll, project management, and team collaboration in one ecosystem. Its flagship product is **Aiwi Team**, an HRMS covering task/project tracking, attendance, leave management, payroll automation, shift scheduling, and employee monitoring. (Note: the brochure's "AI solutions" category label is loose — Aiwi is a business-ops SaaS product, not an AI/ML services firm.)
