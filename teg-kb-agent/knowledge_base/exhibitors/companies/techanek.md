## Overview
TechAnek Technologies is an Ahmedabad-based cloud-native solutions and DevOps consultancy that consults, designs, builds, and manages cloud-native applications and data systems for high-growth startups and agile enterprises. Its core strengths are DevOps, cloud, and containers — services span DevOps consulting, cloud strategy, cloud migration, containerization, and Kubernetes consulting.
