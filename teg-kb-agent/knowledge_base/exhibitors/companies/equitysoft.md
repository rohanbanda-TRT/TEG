## Overview
Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "Equity Soft" sponsor logo to **equitysoft.in**, which was fetched directly and 301-redirects to **equitysofttechnologies.com** — the exact domain already on record in this file, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. **Equitysoft Technologies Pvt Ltd** is an Ahmedabad-based IT Services and IT Consulting company, incorporated in August 2019, offering web and mobile application design/development (PHP, Android, iPhone, Flutter, WordPress, e-commerce, payment gateways, healthcare apps) along with UI/graphics design, SEO, and — per its current site — AI development and workflow automation services.
