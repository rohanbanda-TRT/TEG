## Overview
GavBit Private Limited is an Ahmedabad-based IT and ITeS solution provider ("Beyond the Bit") offering IT consulting, web design and development, mobile app development, cloud management, DevOps, Office 365 management, IT troubleshooting, and IT/web/email/document/IoT security. It also develops cybersecurity products — its first being SimpleDMARC (simpledmarc.com), an email authentication tool.
