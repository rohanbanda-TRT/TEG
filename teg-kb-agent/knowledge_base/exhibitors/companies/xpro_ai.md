## Overview
Xpro.ai is an Ahmedabad-built, bootstrapped event-technology company (founded 2022) providing a white-label event operations platform — branded registration and ticketing, payment gateways, QR entry, badges, exhibitor lead capture, real-time analytics dashboards, and on-ground staffing. It positions itself for Indian conferences, exhibitions, and trade shows, and reports 740,000+ processed check-ins. The name is chosen to sound like "expo."

## Company Details
- **Founded:** 2022.
- **Founder(s) / leadership:** Nilay Khandhar (founder/registrant; also TEG core organizer, Green Apex); **Saumil Patel** — Co-Founder & Director / CTO (also TEG co-organizer; Master's in Computer Science (IT), GLS University; prior: Green Apex, eTechMavens).
- **Team size:** Not confirmed.
- **HQ:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
- **Tech stack / focus:** Event registration & ticketing SaaS, white-label event portals (deployable in 48–72 hours), onsite QR check-in, lead capture, analytics; also a "VisionX" event app.
- **Contact:** via xpro.ai.
