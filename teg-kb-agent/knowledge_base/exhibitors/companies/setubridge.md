## Overview
SetuBridge Technolabs is an Ahmedabad-based e-commerce development agency, founded in 2012 by Sagar Panchal, with over a decade of experience across Magento, Shopify, WooCommerce, and WordPress. It has completed 150+ e-commerce projects, built 30+ Magento extensions and 5 Shopify apps, and serves clients across the USA, UK, Ireland, Australia, and UAE. It was recognized as one of Ahmedabad's best-reviewed e-commerce development partners for 2022 by The Manifest Company Awards.
