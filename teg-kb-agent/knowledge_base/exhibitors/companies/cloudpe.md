## Overview

Exhibitor identity now confirmed: the official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page (techexpogujarat.com/exhibitors) links the "CloudPe" sponsor logo directly to **cloudpe.com**, resolving the prior ambiguity in this file. CloudPe is a full-stack cloud infrastructure platform (compute, managed Kubernetes, GPU cloud, object storage) and is a brand of **Leapswitch Networks Pvt. Ltd.**, a company that began as "LaceHost" in Pune (2006), rebranded to Leapswitch Networks in 2009, and has since expanded into the US, Europe, and Mumbai (from 2014). Direct confirmation from the official TEG exhibitors page settles *which* CloudPe exhibited, but the company's own site states it is Mumbai-based (data centers in Navi Mumbai; expansion planned to Delhi and Chennai) — it is a pan-India cloud provider, not a Gujarat-local company, so it exhibited at TEG as an outside vendor rather than a regional company.

## Company Details

- **Brand of:** Leapswitch Networks Pvt. Ltd.
- **Origin:** Founded as LaceHost in Pune (2006); rebranded Leapswitch Networks (2009)
- **Expansion:** US and Europe data centers; first Indian office opened in Mumbai (2014)
- **Services:** Enterprise-grade compute, managed Kubernetes clusters, GPU cloud, object/data storage — positioned as an India-based alternative to AWS-style hyperscale cloud
