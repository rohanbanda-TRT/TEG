## Overview
factoHR is an AI-powered, cloud-based HRMS and payroll platform automating end-to-end HR operations — payroll, attendance, leave, performance, and workforce management — with an employee self-service mobile app. It is trusted by 3,500+ businesses and 2.6 million employees, including Wipro, Siemens, Tata Steel, GHCL, and Cycle Agarbatti. TEG 2024 speaker **Aniruddh Nagodra** is associated with the company (per `sector_wise_participation.md`).
