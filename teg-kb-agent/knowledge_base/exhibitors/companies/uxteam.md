## Overview

Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "UX Team" sponsor logo directly to **ux.team**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. UXTeam is a B2B, white-label UX/UI design agency providing "structured white-label design partnerships for marketing, tech & event agencies," spanning marketing experience design, e-commerce UX, product/app design, and event experience design. The company is headquartered in Edison, New Jersey, USA, with additional offices in Ahmedabad, India and Dubai, UAE — the Ahmedabad office is the presumed link to Gujarat operations.

## Company Details

- **HQ:** Edison, New Jersey, USA (additional offices: Ahmedabad, India; Dubai, UAE)
- **Team size:** 11–50 employees (29 listed on LinkedIn)
- **Clients served:** United States, Canada, Middle East, Europe, Australia
- **Positioning:** Strategy-first, business-oriented niche UX/UI agency working as a reliable white-label partner for other agencies, rather than direct end-client UX consulting
