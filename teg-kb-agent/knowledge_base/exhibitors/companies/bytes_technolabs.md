## Overview
Bytes TechnoLab (legal name Bytes Technolab Private Limited) is an application design, development, and digital product engineering company headquartered in Ahmedabad, with additional presence in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and Saudi Arabia. Its core expertise spans AI/ML, digital product engineering, MVP development, and web/mobile application development, with a notable focus on serving the logistics and transportation industry with workflow-automation and operational-visibility solutions.

## Company Details
- Founded 2011.
- Registered/headquartered at D-807 The First, B/S Keshavbaug Party Plot, B/H ITC Hotel, Vastrapur, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380015.
- As of May 31, 2025, latest reported employee count: 78.
- International offices/operations in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and Saudi Arabia in addition to India.
