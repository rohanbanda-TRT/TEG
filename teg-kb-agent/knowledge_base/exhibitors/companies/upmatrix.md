## Overview

Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "UpMatrix" sponsor logo directly to **upmatrix.in**, which resolves the earlier "plausible but unconfirmed" status in this file. UpMatrix is a WhatsApp CRM platform enabling businesses to build chatbots, capture leads, manage customer conversations, and run marketing campaigns through WhatsApp. The product is powered by **Matrixhive Technologies** — the same Ahmedabad-registered parent company previously identified as a candidate — confirming that earlier identification was correct.

## Company Details

- **Product:** UpMatrix — WhatsApp Business CRM (upmatrix.in)
- **Parent company:** Matrixhive Technologies Private Limited, Ahmedabad (GST-registered April 21, 2021; annual turnover reported ₹1.5–5 crore)
- **Positioning:** "Made with ♥ in India"
- **Features:** Drag-and-drop chatbot builder, lead management, team inbox, campaign automation, WhatsApp verification, 60+ platform integrations
- **Pricing:** Free tier plus Pro/Business/Enterprise paid tiers, starting ₹1,750/month for Pro
- **Verticals served:** HR, healthcare, real estate, education, e-commerce
- **Note:** A separate, unrelated company "MatrixHive Solutions" (mobility/IoT) also surfaced in earlier searches — not to be confused with Matrixhive Technologies.
