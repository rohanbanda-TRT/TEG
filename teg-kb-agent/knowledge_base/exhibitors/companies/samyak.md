## Overview
Samyak Infotech Pvt Ltd (styled "Samy@k") is an Ahmedabad-based custom software development company. It began in 1991 as an instrumentation company and moved into IT services in 1998, giving it 25+ years in software development. Services span enterprise software, mobile app development, AI/ML solutions, and IT consulting for logistics, healthcare, fintech, and e-commerce. It reports 100+ staff and 50+ clients across 72 countries; ISO 9001:2015 certified and Microsoft-certified.
