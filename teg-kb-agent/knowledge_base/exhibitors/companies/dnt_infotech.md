## Overview
DnT Infotech LLP is a custom software development company based in Ahmedabad, offering custom software development, application testing, IT staff augmentation, and mobile app development. It is a NASSCOM-registered IT services company organized into three main verticals: Digital Product Engineering (new product development, microservices migration, enterprise mobile development, back-end development, cloud/DevOps), Quality Engineering (test automation, performance/load/stress testing, mobile cloud testing, VAPT), and IT Professional Services (staff augmentation).
