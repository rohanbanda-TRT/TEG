## Overview
Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "ECount" sponsor logo directly to **ecount.in**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. This is **eCount Infotech Pvt. Ltd.**, which provides a suite of cloud-based SaaS business-management tools under the "eCount" brand — eCount-Book, ERP, Factory, TMS, Fleet, CRM, HR, and OrderApp — covering accounting, billing, logistics, and operations management. Note the company's headquarters is in Rajkot, Gujarat rather than Ahmedabad, though it markets an expanding presence across Gujarat cities including Ahmedabad, Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, and Chennai — so while exhibitor identity is now confirmed via the official source, this remains a Rajkot-headquartered (not Ahmedabad-local) company.

## Company Details
- Established 2009 as "Epic Developers" (custom app development), later transitioning to financial/operational management software under the "eCount" brand.
- Headquartered in Rajkot, India; expanding network across Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai and other cities.
- Team size: approximately 20+ professionals.
- 3,000+ clients across 100+ industries, including logistics, trading, and the pesticide/fertilizer sector.
