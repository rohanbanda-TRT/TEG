## Overview
The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "Hana" sponsor logo directly to **hanaplatform.com**, which markets itself as a "Rapid Application Development Platform for Web, Mobile Apps." This resolves which "Hana" is the actual TEG exhibitor (ruling out the previously-considered unrelated candidates: HanAra Software, HANA SOFTWARE SOLUTION, HANASOFT Solutions, hana-software.com, hanatechnologies.org). However, the site itself did not yield an about/contact page with a physical address, parent-company name, founding date, or team size in this pass, and a targeted LinkedIn search for "hanaplatform.com"/"Hana platform" + Ahmedabad did not surface a matching company page — so the Ahmedabad/Gujarat location is not yet independently corroborated beyond its listing as a TEG exhibitor.

**Founder (per podcast) — confidence upgrade:** A dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode, "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 13: Jitendra Dadhaniya, Hana" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba), names **Jitendra Dadhaniya** as the founder/leader of "Hana," directly matching the TEG exhibitor entry name. A dedicated founder-named episode in this TEG-branded series is treated as strong independent confirmation, resolving the prior Medium-confidence status.
