## Overview
Miracle Accounting Software is a general-purpose accounting, GST billing, inventory, and e-invoicing product developed by R.K. Softwares (Rkit Software Pvt Ltd), a Rajkot, Gujarat company founded in 1998. It is positioned as one of India's top-10 accounting software providers, scaling from individual accountants to small shops to larger industries, with 20,000+ users. Additional products include Miracle-G, Miracle-Excise, Miracle-DE, Miracle-T, Miracle-Petro, and My Investor.
