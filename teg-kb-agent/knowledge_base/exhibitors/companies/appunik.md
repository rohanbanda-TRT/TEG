## Overview
AppUnik is an IT consulting and custom software development company based in Ahmedabad, specializing in AI, mobile apps, and enterprise solutions, with a team of engineers, AI specialists, and e-commerce experts. Its core service areas include E-Commerce Development, IT Staff Augmentation, Agentic AI, and AI-powered Advertising Solutions, alongside broader offerings in iOS/Android development, UI/UX design, web development, desktop applications, SaaS, blockchain, SEO, QA, and DevOps.
