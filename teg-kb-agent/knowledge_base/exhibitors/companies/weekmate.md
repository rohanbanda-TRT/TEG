## Overview
WeekMate is a cloud-based SaaS suite unifying HRMS, CRM, task/project management, and team communication (real-time chat, encrypted messaging, voice/video, file sharing) in one platform. It targets BPO/KPO and general enterprise operations, and is distinguished by a one-time lifetime license model rather than per-user monthly subscriptions. It reports ISO 27001, AICPA (SOC), and GDPR compliance, 99.9% uptime, and 700+ active users.
