## Overview
BizCompass is a Surat-based (not Ahmedabad) SaaS company offering an all-in-one business automation platform. It bundles recruitment, onboarding, people management, payroll, project management, CRM, and finance into one product, with add-on tools like an activity tracker, API manager, and team chat. Legal entity: Bizcompass Softwares Private Limited (incorporated 17 March 2022; CIN U72900GJ2022PTC130186).
