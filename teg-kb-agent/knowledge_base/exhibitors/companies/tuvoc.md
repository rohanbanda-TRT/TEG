## Overview

Tuvoc Technologies is an Ahmedabad-based IT company (since 2015, per its own site) co-founded by Jitendra Rathod and Manoj Donga, who met as engineering students at L.D. Engineering College, Ahmedabad (2002–2005). Tuvoc builds conventional and "ultra-modern" software solutions incorporating AI, ML, blockchain, AR/VR, automation, and analytics, alongside a specialized line of jewellery billing/inventory software (JewelMaker Girvi Module).
