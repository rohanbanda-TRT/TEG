## Overview
Magneto IT Solutions (listed in TEG's own exhibitor roster as "Magento IT Solutions") is an award-winning Ahmedabad-founded e-commerce agency, in business since 2009. It builds end-to-end B2C, B2B, B2B2B, B2B2C, D2C, and marketplace e-commerce solutions, alongside mobile app development, web development, CMS work, and SEO. The company has grown into a multi-country operation with offices beyond India.

## Company Details
- **Founded:** 2009, by Ronak Meghani, Nitesh Behani, and Bhargav Thakkar (source attribution varies slightly across secondary profiles).
- **Team size:** Roughly 50–250 employees depending on source (Clutch cites 50–249; LinkedIn cites 51–200; one 2025 snapshot cites 152).
- **Locations:** Ahmedabad and Pimpri-Chinchwad (India); also Blacktown, Australia; Thurmaston, England; and Brampton, Canada.
- **Track record:** Reports 1,500+ projects completed for 500+ clients across 20 countries.
