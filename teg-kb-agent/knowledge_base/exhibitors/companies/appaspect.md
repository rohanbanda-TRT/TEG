## Overview
AppAspect Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a mobile app and custom web development company based in Ahmedabad, delivering digital solutions across a wide range of domains including Healthcare, Hospitality, Supply Chain, Fitness, Sports, EdTech, e-commerce, Media/Content/Entertainment, Travel, Social Media, Finance, and Cloud Services. Its own site markets it as a leading Ahmedabad mobile-app-development company, and it directly promoted its own attendance at TEG 2024 via a dedicated event landing page.

## Company Details
- Founded 2011.
- Headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
- Team size: reported inconsistently across third-party sources, roughly 10–28 employees depending on source/date.
- Note: co-organizer Gurpritsingh Saini (AppAspect) is listed as a TEG core organizer in `organizers_team/organizers_and_team.md`, connecting this exhibitor directly to TEG's organizing coalition.

**Founder (per podcast):** Further corroborated by a dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode — "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 45: Gurupritsingh Saini" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba) — which names **Gurupritsingh Saini** as "the visionary leader of AppAspect Technologies Pvt. Ltd.," matching the co-organizer already on record above (minor spelling variant: "Gurpritsingh" vs "Gurupritsingh," clearly the same person).
