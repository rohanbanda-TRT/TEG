## Overview

WanBuffer Services is an Ahmedabad-based Odoo Partner specializing in solution integration and business automation, including AI-enabled Odoo implementations, co-founded and directed by **Jigar Joshi** (also a TEG co-organizer — see `organizers_team/jigar_joshi.md`). WanBuffer describes itself as a global player in the Odoo community with 50+ Odoo business solutions delivered, and operates across the USA, UK, India, UAE, Europe, Kuwait, and France.

## Company Details

- **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India
- **Director & Co-Founder:** Jigar Joshi (MCA, University of Mysore)
- **Co-Founder (per podcast):** Jelam Davda — named alongside the company in "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 9: Jelam Davda, Wan Buffer" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba)
- **Track record:** 50+ Odoo business solutions delivered
- **Service areas:** USA, UK, India, UAE, Europe, Kuwait, France
- **Core specialty:** Odoo Implementation, Customization, and Migration; AI-enabled ERP/business automation

## TEG Participation

Confirmed TEG 2024 exhibitor, extensively documented via WanBuffer's own blog: multiple posts cover "A Comprehensive Look at the Agenda of TechExpo Gujarat 2024," "Discover the Future of Tech at TechExpo Gujarat 2024," "Unlock In-Depth Odoo Knowledge at Tech Expo Gujarat 2024," and "E-Invoicing with Odoo Solutions – TechExpo Ahmedabad Gujarat 2024." At the booth (December 20–21, 2024), WanBuffer showcased Odoo implementation, customization, and migration solutions with a hands-on Odoo demo and experts guiding attendees. Co-founder **Jigar Joshi** is separately one of TEG's co-organizers — see `organizers_team/jigar_joshi.md` for his individual profile; this file documents the company/exhibitor side.
