## Overview
CampusDean is an all-in-one, cloud-based school ERP software product designed to digitalize academic and administrative operations for schools, colleges, and educational institutions — covering admissions, fee management, exam scheduling, report-card generation, and more. It is a product of Unnati Informatics LLP, an Ahmedabad-based IT solutions provider. It markets itself as one of India's most widely trusted school management software platforms and supports Android access.

## Company Details
- Parent company Unnati Informatics LLP founded 2005, based in Ahmedabad, India.
- Core product modules: Admission Management, Fee Management, Exam Management, plus broader student information, academics, finance, and communication features on a single cloud platform.
