## Overview
BetterBugs is an Ahmedabad-based, bootstrapped SaaS company (founded 2023) that makes a visual bug-reporting and debugging tool. Its browser extension and web app let testers and developers capture a bug in one click — screenshot/screen recording plus auto-attached developer logs (console, network, device/environment) and AI-assisted debug context — and push it straight to Jira, Slack, GitHub, and similar. Legal entity: QAVERSE Technology LLP.
