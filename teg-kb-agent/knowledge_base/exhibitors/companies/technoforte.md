## Overview

Technoforte (Technoforte Software Private Limited) is an ISO 9001:2015 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022-certified IT company offering Managed IT Services, Digital Transformation Services, and IT Outsourcing/Consultancy. Founded in 2001 by four professionals, the company is headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, with partner offices across the USA, Europe, Middle East, and APAC — it is not itself an Ahmedabad/Gujarat-headquartered firm, but exhibited at TEG 2024 in Ahmedabad.

## Company Details

- **Founded:** 2001
- **HQ:** 3rd Floor, VRR Royal Commercial Complex, HSR Layout, Bengaluru, Karnataka
- **Certifications:** ISO 9001:2015, ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- **Industries served:** Manufacturing, Automotive, Logistics, Warehousing, BFSI, Retail, Supply Chain
- **Flagship product:** PALMS™ — a warehouse management system using barcode, RFID, and voice-picking technology, with inventory forecasting, real-time dashboards, and ERP integration

## TEG Participation

Technoforte exhibited at **TEG 2024** (December 20–21, 2024, Vigyan Bhavan Science City, Ahmedabad) at **Booth E84**. Their own event landing page confirms two showcase areas: the PALMS™ Warehouse Management System, and their professional IT services (managed IT, independent verification & validation, IT staff augmentation, strategic IT consultancy).
