## Overview
Chronox AI builds no-code AI assistants and agents using generative AI (OpenAI API / ChatGPT), letting businesses deploy conversational agents across websites, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and other channels. Agents handle inquiries, orders, returns, and other business actions, with knowledge-base management, real-time data integration, and social-media integration. It reports 30+ business customers across logistics, real estate, e-commerce, translation, and finance.
