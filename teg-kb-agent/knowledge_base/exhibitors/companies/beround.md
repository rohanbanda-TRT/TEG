## Overview
Beround is a global IT consultancy specializing in AI, testing, and "transition & transformation," with subsidiaries in the Netherlands, USA, and India. Client-facing delivery, project management, and solution design run from the Netherlands; engineering, QA, and AI development are executed by the India team. Core capabilities span Salesforce and Zoho CRM implementation, end-to-end testing/QA, AI model training, and AI automation. It brands itself "a smile-enabling company."
