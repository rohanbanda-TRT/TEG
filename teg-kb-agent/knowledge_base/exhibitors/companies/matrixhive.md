## Overview
MatrixHive Technologies Pvt Ltd is an Ahmedabad-based product engineering studio and IT consultancy founded in 2016. It builds its own SaaS products and deploys AI for enterprises, alongside services in mobile app development, web/CMS, DevOps, UI/UX, and QA. Client industries include education, enterprise, real estate, and e-commerce.
