## Overview
Ecosmob Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is an Ahmedabad-headquartered company specializing in AI-powered VoIP and real-time communication systems, including custom VoIP development (FreeSWITCH, WebRTC, Asterisk, Kamailio, OpenSIPS), QA services, AI/ML solutions (chatbots, voicebots, sentiment analysis), DevOps consulting, and enterprise communication infrastructure such as softswitches, session border controllers, and contact center solutions.

## Company Details
- Founded 2006 (one source says 2007) by Ruchir Brahmbhatt and Maulik Shah.
- Operates globally with offices in India (primary development hub), Canada, and the USA.
- Leadership: Maulik Shah (Co-Founder & CEO), Ruchir Brahmbhatt (Co-Founder & CTO), Krunal Patel (Chief Revenue Officer), Sagar Malam (Chief Innovation Officer), Khushal Fadiya (Chief Growth Officer – Products).
- 750+ clients worldwide; 2,500+ enterprise deployments across 40+ countries.
- ISO 9001:2015 certified; rated 4.3/5 on G2, 4.7/5 on Gartner, 4.4/5 on Trustpilot.
