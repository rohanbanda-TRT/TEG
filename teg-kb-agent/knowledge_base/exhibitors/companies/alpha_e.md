## Overview
Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page (techexpogujarat.com/exhibitors) links the "Alpha E Barcode" sponsor logo directly to **alphaebarcode.com**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. This is **Alpha E Barcode Solutions Private Limited**, an Ahmedabad-based company with roughly 25 years in the software industry, which in May 2026 rebranded to "Alpha ERP Solutions Private Limited." It provides ERP, POS, barcode, and business software for retailers, wholesalers, manufacturers, jewellery stores, supermarkets, restaurants and other industries, and has products like "G-Soft Extreme Wholesale ERP" for wholesale distributors.

## Company Details
- Ahmedabad-based, ~25 years in the software industry (implies founding in the late 1990s/early 2000s), per its own recent rebrand announcement.
- Product lines: G-Soft Extreme Wholesale ERP (Inventory Management, Financial Accounting, Distribution Management, Web Reporting), plus a newer cloud-based Web ERP platform incorporating AI, RFID, automation, and digital security features.
- Industries served: retail, wholesale, manufacturing, jewellery, supermarkets, restaurants.
