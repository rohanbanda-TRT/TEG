## Overview
Fintegration (branded FintegrationFS, legal/operating name "MobileFirst Applications | Fintegration") is an Ahmedabad-headquartered fintech software development company building custom fintech products — invoice financing, credit automation, underwriting, BNPL mobile apps, lending platforms, and investment applications — for fintech clients globally, including named clients NSDL, Flipkart, Xoxoday, and Fold Money.

## Company Details
- The firm began in 2014/2015 as a general software-consulting group ("MobileFirst"), pivoting fully to fintech in 2021 under the Fintegration/FintegrationFS brand.
- Headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India (Ambli area, per JustDial listing).
- 51–200 employees.
- Services: fintech software development, MVP development, API integration, and solutions across accounting, banking, payments, insurance, investment, lending, and compliance.
- Recently launched a fintech API discovery platform for India (per Business Standard press coverage, 2023).
