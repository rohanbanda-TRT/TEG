## Overview
Sheth Info is an Ahmedabad-based dealer in refurbished IT hardware — desktops, laptops, workstations, servers, and Apple products, for sale and rental — established in 2006. It also does repair and maintenance of laptops and PCs. Customers include IT firms, manufacturers, educational institutions, healthcare centres, and BPO/KPO companies. Positioning: "Reliable · Serviceable · Refurbished," with a sustainability angle (extending hardware life, reducing e-waste).
