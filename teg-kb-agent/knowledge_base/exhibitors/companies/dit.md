## Overview
Identified with confidence. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "DIT" sponsor logo directly to **ditinteractive.com** — the exhibitor is **DIT Interactive Pvt Ltd**, an Ahmedabad-headquartered digital agency (privately held since 1999) offering eCommerce development, web development, UI/UX design, growth marketing, and agentic AI services. Note this remains a distinct entity from "Dev IT Simplified" (separate verified exhibitor, `dev_it_simplified.md`) and from "Dev Information Technology Limited" — neither should be conflated with DIT Interactive.
