## Overview
Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "Delta Infosoft" sponsor logo directly to **deltainfosoft.com**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status despite "Delta" being a highly generic name. This is **Delta Infosoft Pvt Ltd**, an Ahmedabad-based software development company providing ERP software, CRM solutions, Zoho CRM implementation, and custom business automation software for small and mid-size businesses, with custom ERP solutions built for manufacturing, trading, and service industries across Gujarat.

**Founder (per podcast):** Independently corroborated by a dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode — "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 29: Sandip Mevada" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba) — which names **Sandip Mevada** as the founder/leader of "Delta InfoSoft Pvt. Ltd.," matching this company exactly.

## Company Details
- Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.
- Focus: ERP, CRM (including Zoho CRM implementation), and custom business automation software.
- Target industries: manufacturing, trading, and service businesses across Gujarat.
- No confirmed founding year or team size was found.
