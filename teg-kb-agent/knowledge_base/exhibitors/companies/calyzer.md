## Overview
Callyzer is an Ahmedabad-based, cloud-based SIM/call-log-based telecalling analytics and lead-management platform. Its mobile app syncs with device call logs to give real-time visibility into inbound/outbound calling activity for sales and telecalling teams, with detailed reports, real-time call monitoring, CRM integration, and daily status reports.

> **Naming note:** The brochure slug used here is "calyzer"; the company's own branding and its TEG 2024 sponsor listing use "Callyzer" (callyzer.co). Treated as the same entity. Category noted in the task as "field-force & attendance-tracking" — public sources describe it more specifically as telecalling/call-tracking analytics for field sales teams.
