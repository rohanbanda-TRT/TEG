## Overview
Codevision Technologies Private Limited is a custom software development company based in Ahmedabad that empowers global enterprises with AI-driven solutions, specializing in Microsoft technologies — including Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Power Platform, Microsoft Teams, and Power BI — alongside broader software product engineering, application development, and cloud services aimed at digital transformation. Clients span healthcare, finance, transportation, and manufacturing.

## Company Details
- Founded 2018, 11–50 employees.
- Directors: Ashish Maganlal Chotalia and Jinesh Pravinbhai Shah.
- Registered address: 1307–1310, Colonnade-2, Rajpath Rangoli Road, opposite AMC parking, Ahmedabad City, Gujarat 380054.
- Member of the Ahmedabad IT Management Executive Delegation (AIMED) — the same coalition that organizes TEG.
