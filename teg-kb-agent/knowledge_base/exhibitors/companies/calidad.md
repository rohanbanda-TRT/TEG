## Overview
Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "Calidad Infotech" sponsor logo directly to **calidadinfotech.com**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. **Calidad Infotech** is a software quality solutions provider headquartered in Ahmedabad that helps businesses improve software quality without extending time-to-production, via Software Testing and on-demand Cloud/DevOps services. Its specialties include Quality, DevOps, Cloud, Automation, Load Testing, and Performance testing, with a stated aim of cutting QA costs by up to 50%.

**Founder (per podcast):** Independently corroborated by a dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode — "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 19: Samay Thakkar, Calidad" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba) — which names **Samay Thakkar** as the founder/leader of Calidad.

## Company Details
- Founded 2021, headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
