## Overview
Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "Satva Solutions" sponsor logo directly to **satvasolutions.com**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. Satva Solutions is an Ahmedabad-based accounting integration and fintech product development company, founded in 2013 by Chintan Prajapati. It specializes in accounting system integrations (QuickBooks Desktop, Xero), third-party API/B2B/X12-protocol integration, and MIS dashboards. While "Satva Solutions" is a specific and plausible Ahmedabad IT-sector match for the TEG 2024 exhibitor listed simply as "Satva," this identification was not independently confirmed via a TEG-specific source, hence Medium rather than Verified confidence.

## Company Details
- **Founded:** 2013, by Chintan Prajapati.
- **Location:** Headquartered near Science City Road, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.
- **Team size:** 51–200 employees, with staff reportedly across Asia, South America, and Europe.
- **Specialties:** Accounting integrations, FinTech product development, Microsoft .NET, Microsoft Azure, ERP integration, QuickBooks Desktop and Xero integration, MIS dashboards, third-party/B2B/X12 protocol integration.
