## Overview
Samcom Technobrains Pvt. Ltd. is an Ahmedabad-based software development company with 14+ years of experience, specializing in mobile app (Android, iPhone) and web development (.NET, PHP, Joomla). It was founded by Samir Khatri and has built a client base of 250+ worldwide. Note: despite the "Technobrains" portion of its name, this is a distinct company from **TechnoBrains Business Solutions LLP** (the separate TEG 2024 exhibitor / TEG core-organizer Bhavik Shah's company) — the name overlap appears coincidental/unrelated based on available sources, not a corporate affiliation.

## Company Details
- **Founder:** Samir Khatri.
- **Location:** B-Wing, 521/22/23, Venus Stratum, Nehrunagar, Ahmedabad – 380015, Gujarat.
- **Team size:** 166 employees (per one revenue/headcount aggregator).
- **Revenue:** Reported at approximately $6.3 million (per aggregator estimate).
- **Track record:** 250+ clients worldwide; 14+ years of IT software development experience.
- **Specialties:** IT consulting, brand creation, on-demand software development; mobile (Android/iPhone) and web (.NET, PHP, Joomla) development.
