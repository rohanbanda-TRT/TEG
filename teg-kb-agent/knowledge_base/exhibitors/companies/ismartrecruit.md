## Overview
iSmartRecruit is a cloud-based AI recruitment platform combining an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) and Recruiting CRM in a single workspace, aimed at staffing firms, recruitment agencies, executive search firms, and in-house HR teams. It reports a presence in 70+ countries with support for 14 languages, and bundles AI matching, resume parsing, generative/conversational AI, compliance management, and candidate management.
