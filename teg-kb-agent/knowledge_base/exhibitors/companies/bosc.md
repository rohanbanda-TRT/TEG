## Overview
Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "BOSC Tech Labs" sponsor logo directly to **bosctechlabs.com**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. This is **BOSC Tech Labs Private Limited**, a Gandhinagar-based (greater Ahmedabad area) software company driven by "creativity, passion, latest trends, and innovation," offering custom software development, web application development, CMS development, and static/dynamic/blogging website development. The company markets itself today primarily as an AI software & solutions developer for SMB/mid-market teams.

## Company Details
- Incorporated December 13, 2019, as BOSC TECH LABS PRIVATE LIMITED.
- Based in Sargasan, Gandhinagar, Gujarat, with an office at Kudasan, Gandhinagar.
- Recognised as a DPIIT startup.
- Revenue of INR 5.49 Cr for FY ending March 31, 2025 (1-year revenue CAGR of 27%, EBITDA CAGR of 23%).
- Leadership: Maheshkumar Pesumal Lalwani and Rashmi Maheshkumar Lalwani (directors).
