## Overview

Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "VisCap" sponsor logo directly to **viscap-cs.com**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. VISCAP Consultancy Services is a SAP implementation and support provider established in 2013, with a registered office in Portland, Oregon (USA) and delivery centers in Mumbai and Ahmedabad, India. The company specializes in SAP-powered digital transformation — SAP S/4HANA, SuccessFactors, and integrated HCM payroll — serving consumer products, pharma, and manufacturing clients.

**Founder (per podcast):** Independently corroborated by a dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode — "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 35: Dinesh Khunt" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba) — which names **Dinesh Khunt** as the founder/leader of "VISCAP Consultancy Services Pvt. Ltd.," matching this company exactly.
