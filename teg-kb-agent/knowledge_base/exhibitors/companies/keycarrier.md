## Overview
Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "Key Carrier" sponsor logo directly to **key-cms.com**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. Key Carrier Management Service Pvt. Ltd. (branded "Key-CMS") is an Ahmedabad-based outsourcing services provider offering BPO, KPO, RPO, offshore accounting, virtual bookkeeping, remote staffing, and digital marketing/SEO services to clients in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and India. The exhibitor directory categorizes it generically as "Software Dev" — Key-CMS's actual business is closer to BPO/outsourcing/staffing than software development, so treat the category tag with some caution. Note also: a same/similar-named "Key Carrier Group, Co" (keycarrier.co) is an unrelated North American **truck transportation** company — not to be confused with this entity.

**Founder (per podcast):** Independently corroborated by a dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode — "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 49: Tushar Sharma" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba) — which names **Tushar Sharma** as the "leader behind Key Carrier Management Service Pvt. Ltd.," matching this company exactly.
