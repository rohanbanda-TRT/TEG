## Overview
Essence Solusoft is an Ahmedabad-based full-stack software development and IT consulting company founded in 2016, building scalable web and mobile applications for startups, enterprises, and SMEs. It is known for Ruby on Rails development alongside AI/ML solutions, modern front-end frameworks, e-commerce development, and DevOps. It reports 20+ engineers, 130+ projects, and clients in real estate, healthcare, and e-commerce.
