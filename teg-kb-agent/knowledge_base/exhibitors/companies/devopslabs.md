## Overview
DevOpsLabs is a "Singapore-born" IT products and services company focused on DevOps transformation and software delivery excellence. Services include DevSecOps implementation, custom application development, platform engineering, test automation, and managed services, plus consulting in SRE, digital transformation, and process re-engineering. Its key product is **Exora**, a "Value Stream Delivery platform for SDLC Excellence." Clients span enterprises, startups, and mission-critical organizations.
