## Overview
Metizsoft Solutions Pvt Ltd is an Ahmedabad-based web, mobile, and e-commerce development / IT services company, founded in 2012 by **Manthan Bhavsar**, who is also a co-organizer of Tech Expo Gujarat (per `organizers_team/manthan_bhavsar.md`). Manthan Bhavsar has 12+ years of experience in e-commerce, fintech, and platform engineering, previously holding business-development roles at Sigma Solve and Samvith Technologies. He also hosts the podcast "Chai Time with ManthanBaba," on which fellow TEG organizers have appeared as guests. **Chetan Sheladiya** (Founder & CEO of Metizsoft, per the company's own blog author page and separately corroborated by his LinkedIn/ZoomInfo profiles) is a second co-founder alongside Manthan Bhavsar — the two hold Founder & CEO and Founder & CMO roles respectively. In Metizsoft's own "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024" episode (Ep 4, hosted by co-founder Manthan Bhavsar himself), Chetan Sheladiya is the featured guest discussing the company's journey.

## Company Details
- **Founded:** 2012.

## TEG 2024 Participation
