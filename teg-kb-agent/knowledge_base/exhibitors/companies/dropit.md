## Overview
Dropit is an Ahmedabad-based on-demand delivery and rides platform operated by Easyexpress Delivery Solutions LLP, launched in 2025. It positions itself around last-mile logistics ("Deliver with Ease") — parcel and passenger movement with seamless booking, real-time tracking, and a range of vehicle options (cycle, two-wheeler, three-wheeler, truck). The service runs through Android and iOS consumer apps plus a "Dropit Pilot" driver app.
