## Overview
TechAlmas LLP is an Ahmedabad-based IT services and consulting company delivering SaaS products and custom web and mobile applications. Its work spans website development, mobile app development (including Flutter), customized online payment solutions, PHP/Laravel, and React/Next.js front-end development. The company reports roughly 22 employees.
