## Overview
Connecti5 is a smart contact-management app that maps a professional's saved network onto a private, location-aware map — importing and organizing contacts, scanning business cards, finding nearby connections, and planning client visits and follow-ups around events and travel. It is built and operated by the ThinkTanker team (led by TEG core organizer **Rajan Rawal**), turning static contact lists into location-aware networks. Free to start; available on iOS, Android, and web.
