## Overview
CirrOps (also styled CirrOps Solutions) is a Cloud and DevOps solutions provider, founded by Nirav Pancholi, focused on CI/CD automation — build, test, and deployment automation for rapid, reliable software delivery. CirrOps directly promoted its own participation in TEG 2024 via its official X (Twitter) account, making it one of the most directly confirmed exhibitors in this batch.

## Company Details
- Founder: Nirav Pancholi.
- One source describes CirrOps Solutions as based in Surat, Gujarat, and notes the company shut down its software-development wing in October 2021 to focus solely on cloud technologies, rebranding as "Wowl Cloud Ops LLP" and becoming an official AWS partner in April 2022 — team scaled from 3 to 9 engineers by end of 2023, with 40+ completed projects across Surat, Mumbai, North America, Europe, Middle East, Oceania, and Vietnam. This rebrand detail sits oddly alongside CirrOps' own December 2024 promotion of its TEG 2024 booth under the "Cirrops" name — the two facts were not reconciled in this research pass and may reflect the same entity continuing to operate under both names, or two distinct entities.

## TEG 2024 Participation
Listed as a named TEG 2024 exhibitor (Software Dev category) in `exhibitors_directory.md`. Directly confirmed via CirrOps' own X post: "Boost your software delivery with CI/CD! Join Cirrops at Tech Expo Gujarat 2024 to explore how we automate build, test, & deployment for rapid, reliable results. Where: Vigyan Bhavan, Ahmedabad. When: 20th–21st Dec 2024. Booth No: E-43."
