## Overview
Eternal Web Private Limited is an AWS Advanced Tier Consulting Partner and certified Odoo partner headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, with a second office in Newcastle upon Tyne, UK. Founded in 2013, it specializes in AI-driven AWS cloud consulting, generative AI application development (branded "Eternal AI Studio"), DevOps, and custom enterprise/Odoo ERP software. This company is directly tied to TEG core organizer **Nirav Shah** (Director) — see `organizers_team/nirav_shah.md` for his individually researched profile, which this entry builds on rather than re-deriving from scratch.

## Company Details
- Founded 2013.
- Headquarters: 401, Satyam Mall, B/H Kameshwar School, Jodhpur Cross Road, Satellite, Ahmedabad, Gujarat – 380015, India; second office in Newcastle upon Tyne, UK.
- Fewer than 50 employees (per company database listings).
- Services: cloud-native application development, generative AI, enterprise platforms, AWS solutions, product design/development, software re-engineering, QA, digital transformation consulting, custom enterprise software, and Odoo ERP implementation.
