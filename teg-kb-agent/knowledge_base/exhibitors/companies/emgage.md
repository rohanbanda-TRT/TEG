## Overview
Emgage is an Ahmedabad-headquartered HRMS (Human Resource Management System) SaaS company, founded in 2018 and operated by Oases Technovations LLP. It provides a customizable HR platform automating the employee lifecycle — onboarding through separation — aimed primarily at SMEs and startups in India. Its confirmed address is 111-112, 1st Floor, Ashirwad Paras, Corporate Rd, Prahlad Nagar, Ahmedabad – 380015 (per emgage.work and web search).

**Discrepancy flagged in this pass:** The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page (techexpogujarat.com/exhibitors) links its "Emgage" sponsor logo to **emgage.com** — but emgage.com was fetched directly and is a completely unrelated Pasadena, California-based cybersecurity/compliance (CMMC, PCI DSS, HIPAA, FedRAMP) platform, with no mention of Ahmedabad, Gujarat, or HR software. This is almost certainly either a stale/incorrect link on TEG's own exhibitors page, or two same-named companies exist and TEG's page happens to link the wrong one. Given this contradiction, confidence is kept at Medium rather than upgraded to Verified — the Ahmedabad HRMS "Emgage" (emgage.work) remains the far more plausible actual exhibitor by name/sector/geography fit, but the official source's link does not corroborate it.
