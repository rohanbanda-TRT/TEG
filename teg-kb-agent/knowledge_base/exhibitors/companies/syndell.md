## Overview
Syndell Technologies is an Ahmedabad-headquartered software development company with branches in the USA and UK, offering web application development, open-source customization, portal development, e-commerce site development, mobile application development, website design, SEO/SEM, and staff augmentation. It reports 600+ clients across 20+ countries and 5 continents.

## Company Details
- **Incorporated:** 25 Dec 2021 (per company registry), though the company markets itself as in business since 2014.
- **Location:** 304, Shikhar Complex, Srimali Society, Navrangpura, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India – 380009.
- **Team size:** 51–200 employees; company states 50+ full-time specialists.
- **Track record:** 600+ clients, presence in 20+ countries.
- **Specialties:** Custom software development, web application development, mobile app development, e-commerce development, IT outsourcing/staff augmentation.

**Founder (per podcast):** A dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode, "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 6: Tejas Sanghvi, Syndell" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba), names **Tejas Sanghvi** as the founder/leader of Syndell.
