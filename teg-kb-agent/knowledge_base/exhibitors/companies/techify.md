## Overview
Techify Solutions Pvt Ltd is an Ahmedabad-headquartered IT consulting and product-engineering company founded in 2016, with a secondary US office in Sheridan, Wyoming. It focuses on product engineering, software development, DevOps automation, CRM/ERP integration, and AI-powered web and mobile app development, serving education, healthcare, automobile, retail, logistics, and finance clients. Its founder-director **Taral Shah** is one of the twelve core organizers of Tech Expo Gujarat.

## Company Details
- **Founded:** 2016 (HQ Bodakdev, Ahmedabad).
- **Founder(s) / leadership:** Taral Shah — Director / Co-Founder (AWS-certified systems architect, 15+ years IT; also TEG core organizer and co-founder of Lomoso Solutions).
- **Team size:** ~51–200 employees.
- **HQ:** Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, Gujarat; US office in Sheridan, Wyoming.
- **Tech stack / focus:** Product engineering, DevOps automation, cloud (AWS Consulting & Networking Partner), CRM/ERP integration, AI web/mobile. In-house products: AI CardVault, CloudLaunchPad.
- **Recognition:** Vibrant Gujarat Summit 2018 Grand Challenge winner; Gujarat Innovation Society "Trend Setter" award.
- **Contact:** via techifysolutions.com.
