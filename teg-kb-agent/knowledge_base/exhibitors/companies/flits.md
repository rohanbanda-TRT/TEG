## Overview
Flits (operating under the brand name eFANTOM, a registered partnership firm) is an Ahmedabad-based SaaS company, founded in 2017, providing a customer-retention platform for Shopify merchants — covering loyalty programs, referrals, wishlists, passwordless login, and customer accounts to help Shopify brands drive repeat purchases.

## Company Details
- Founded 2017.
- Principal office: #307, North Plaza, Visat-Gandhinagar Highway, Motera, Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
- 14 employees (as of mid-2024, per Tracxn).
- Core products: Flits Loyalty & Rewards, Flits Wishlist ("Back In Stock"), customer accounts, and passwordless login — all distributed via the Shopify App Store.
