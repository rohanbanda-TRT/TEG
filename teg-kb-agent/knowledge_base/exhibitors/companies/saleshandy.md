## Overview
Saleshandy is an Ahmedabad-founded cold email outreach and sales-engagement SaaS platform. It began (2015) as an email tracking tool, pivoted to cold email automation in 2020, and is now an all-in-one outreach platform: a Lead Finder (800M+ contacts), automated email sequences with AI copy, email warm-up and inbox-placement testing, a unified reply inbox, and a Kanban CRM. It is bootstrapped (no outside funding) and holds SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001:2022, and GDPR certifications. G2 rating ~4.6/5 from ~750 reviews.
