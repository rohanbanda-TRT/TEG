## Overview
Serpent Consulting Services Pvt. Ltd. (branded SerpentCS) is an ISO 9001:2008-certified Odoo Gold Partner founded in December 2011, headquartered in the Ahmedabad/Gandhinagar area of Gujarat. It is one of the top contributors to the Odoo open-source ERP/CRM community, having developed 50+ business applications on the platform, and provides Odoo (plus ERPNext, Zoho, and SAP) implementation, customization, consulting, development, migration, and integration services across hospitality, education, healthcare, and manufacturing industries.
