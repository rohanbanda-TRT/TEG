## Overview
TechSofya (Techsofya Digital Private Limited) is an IT and digital technology company established in 2015, primarily focused on specialized services for Yardi (real estate management/investment software) clients, alongside technology consulting, web development, digital marketing, enterprise application management, business process automation, and e-commerce development. Its consultants also cover MRI, Oracle Financials, and SAP for real estate accounting.

## Company Details
- **Founded:** 2015 (Techsofya Digital Private Limited incorporated 16 July 2018).
- **Founder(s) / leadership:** Directors Yogesh Jayantilal Shah and Jaykumar Vishwanath Panicker.
- **Team size:** Not confirmed.
- **HQ:** India (company registered in Mumbai); operational hubs in UAE, India, Netherlands, Singapore, and the UK.
- **Tech stack / focus:** Yardi implementation and support, real estate accounting systems (MRI, Oracle, SAP), web development, automation, e-commerce.
