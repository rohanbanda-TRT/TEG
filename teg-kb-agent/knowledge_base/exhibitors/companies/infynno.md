## Overview
Infynno Solutions LLP is an Ahmedabad-based software development agency, founded in 2017, helping startups and growing businesses build scalable MVPs, SaaS platforms, web apps, and mobile apps — including AI solutions using OpenAI and generative AI (chatbots, voice agents).

## Company Details
- Founded 2017.
- Located at E-720, Ganesh Glory 11, Nr BSNL Office, Jagatpur-Chenpur Road, Sarkhej-Gandhinagar Hwy, Gota, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 382481.
- 48 employees (as of June 2026); 30+ in-house dev team.
- Tech stack specialties: React.js, Next.js, Node.js, Laravel, Tailwind CSS, AWS, React Native.
- Client sectors include education, ridesharing, and media.
