## Overview
MindQuad Solutions Pvt Ltd is an Ahmedabad-based ERP solutions provider, founded in 2000, holding dual certification as a Microsoft Gold Partner and SAP Silver Partner. It bills itself as the only partner in western India dealing in both SAP Business One and Microsoft Dynamics 365, offering CRM consulting, IT staff augmentation, big data, and ERP consulting/system integration.

## Company Details
- **Founded:** 2000.
- **Location:** Plot No. 110, Block A, Ganesh Meridian, Sarkhej-Gandhinagar Highway, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380060.
- **Specialties:** SAP Business One (B1), SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Dynamics 365 (Business Central, Finance & Operations), Microsoft Dynamics CRM.
- **Experience:** Team reports 50+ combined man-years implementing MS Dynamics NAV across chemical manufacturing, engineering, pharma distribution, services, and wine-distribution verticals.
