## Overview
Emblit.ai is an AI lead-qualification platform for real estate teams. Its AI agent ("Ember") reads WhatsApp conversations to extract budget, timeline, intent, and location, scores leads with a "Lead Closing Probability" model, sends instant replies, and books meetings. It integrates with WhatsApp / Meta Lead Ads and website leads (API and CSV), and is initially built for UAE real estate with stated plans to expand to India and B2B SaaS.
