## Overview
Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "Neura Monks" sponsor logo directly to **neuramonks.com**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status and independently corroborating the organizer cross-reference. This is **Neuramonks** — an enterprise AI and deep-tech consulting firm (a brand under Kshatrainfotech Pvt Ltd) headquartered in Ahmedabad, founded and led by **Upendrasinh Zala**, who is a co-organizer of Tech Expo Gujarat (per `organizers_team/upendrasinh_zala.md`). Neuramonks specializes in AI/ML consultancy, RAG systems, LLM development, and MLOps support.

**Note on spelling/capitalization variance:** the exhibitor list spells the name "NeuraMonks" while the company itself is spelled "Neuramonks" (and TEG's own exhibitors page displays it as "Neura Monks"). This is the same discrepancy already flagged in `sector_wise_participation.md` — treated as cosmetic, not a different entity.

## Company Details
- **Founder & CEO:** Upendrasinh Zala, over a decade of IT experience; prior ventures reportedly include Hawkium (digital marketing) and roles at Kshatrainfotech and Winspire Web Solution.
- **Parent entity:** Kshatrainfotech Pvt Ltd.
- **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.
- **Specialties:** AI/ML consultancy, RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) systems, LLM development, MLOps support, data science, NLP.
- **Public speaking:** Upendrasinh Zala spoke on AI in pharma at a GCCI (Gujarat Chamber of Commerce and Industry) session in November 2025.
