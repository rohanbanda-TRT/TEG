## Overview

Confirmed. The official Tech Expo Gujarat exhibitors page links the "WebAppRise" sponsor logo directly to **webapprise.com**, resolving the earlier Medium-confidence status. Webapprise Technologies is an Ahmedabad-based, unfunded/bootstrapped digital solutions provider founded in 2012, offering web development, mobile app development, and digital marketing services to a client base spanning startups through Fortune 500 companies.

**Founder (per podcast):** Independently corroborated by a dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode — "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 40: Avnish Geveriya, Webapprise" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba) — which names **Avnish Geveriya** as the founder/leader of Webapprise.
