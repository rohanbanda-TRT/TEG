## Overview
Green Apex Solutions is an Ahmedabad-founded web and app development company, founded in 2014 by **Nilay Khandhar** (Founder & CEO, and a TEG core organizer — see `organizers_team/nilay_khandhar.md`). It has scaled from a local Ahmedabad shop into an operation spanning 22+ countries, specializing in AI-driven digital product development, headless CMS, custom app development, and IT staffing.

## Company Details
- Founded 2014.
- Headquarters: 6th Floor, Ananta Abode & Space, Opp. Ganesh Genesis, Jagatpur, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 382470; additional offices in Canada, USA, and Australia; also cited as reaching Houston, TX for US business development.
- 140–150+ employees/developers, including certified scrum masters and solution architects.
- 10+ years of experience; services include UI/UX, mobile app development, web app development, AI development, enterprise mobility, and headless CMS (including Angular development).
- Founder & CEO Nilay Khandhar: 12+ years executive experience in business transformation and global expansion (per TEG's own team page).
