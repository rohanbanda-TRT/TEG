## Overview
JigNect Technologies is a software testing company based in Ahmedabad, India, providing comprehensive QA solutions — manual, automated, performance, security, compatibility, usability, and AI/ML testing. It serves clients across healthcare, e-commerce, and BFSI industries, offering QA staff augmentation, test-case/report creation, and automation testing in Selenium, Cypress, and Playwright.

## Company Details
- **Location:** Headquartered in Ahmedabad, India, with an additional office in San Francisco, USA.
- **Team size:** 11–50 employees (Clutch/ZoomInfo).
- **Specialties:** Manual and automated QA testing, performance/security/compatibility/usability testing, AI/ML testing, API testing.

**Founder (per podcast):** A dedicated TEG-branded podcast episode, "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024 | Ep 41: Piyush Patel, Jignect" (Chai Time with ManthanBaba), names **Piyush Patel** as the founder/leader of Jignect.
