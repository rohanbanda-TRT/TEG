## Overview
MagnusMinds IT Solution is an Ahmedabad-based IT outsourcing and software development company specializing in .NET, ASP.NET Core, Power BI, and data engineering/analytics consulting, with 15+ years of operation in Ahmedabad. It is founded and led by **Tejas Shah**, who is also one of the twelve core organizers of Tech Expo Gujarat itself — MagnusMinds is directly tied to TEG's own organizing coalition, not merely an unaffiliated exhibitor.
