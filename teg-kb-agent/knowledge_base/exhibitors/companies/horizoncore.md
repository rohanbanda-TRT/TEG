## Overview
HorizonCore (formally Horizoncore Infosoft) is an Ahmedabad-based custom software development company founded/led by **Pinakin Soni**, Founder, Director & CTO, who is also a TEG core organizer (see `organizers_team/pinakin_soni.md`). It delivers web applications, mobile platforms (iOS, Android, cross-platform), enterprise solutions, e-commerce platforms, UI/UX design, API integration, cloud consulting, and BI/analytics services.

## Company Details
- Company site cites "13+ years of industry leadership"; Pinakin Soni has held his current role since 2022 per RocketReach (figures not fully reconciled — see `organizers_team/pinakin_soni.md`).
- Headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India; global presence referenced in the USA, India, Canada, and the Netherlands.
- Scale (per company site): 600+ projects delivered, 50+ mobile applications built, 50+ team members, 150+ clients across 22+ countries, 75% recurring-client rate.
- Industries served: healthcare, finance, e-commerce, real estate.
- Founder Pinakin Soni: Certified Microsoft Professional with stated expertise in enterprise architecture, cloud, AI/ML, IoT strategy, and blockchain consultation; prior roles at Hidden Brains Infotech, iHorizonTech Consulting, and Hirtechnology.
