## Overview
HK Infosoft is an Ahmedabad-based custom IT solutions company, founded in 2014, building web, cloud, mobile, and generative-AI applications for clients across the USA, Canada, Europe, and Australia.

## Company Details
- Founded 2014.
- Located at 606, Suvas Scala, Nr. Parikh Hospitals, S P Ring Road, Nikol, Ahmedabad, 382350, India.
- Team of 25 engineers (11–50 employees per broader company-database range).
- Services: web app development, mobile app development, graphics/web design, app maintenance, generative AI development, and data analytics.
- Track record: 250+ satisfied clients, 500+ projects.
