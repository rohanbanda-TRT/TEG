## Overview
iValue InfoSolutions (iValue Group) is a Bangalore-headquartered value-added distributor and technology enabler founded in 2008, operating across India, Southeast Asia, and the SAARC region. It aggregates and delivers solutions in cybersecurity, data centre infrastructure, hybrid cloud management, application lifecycle management, information lifecycle management, OT, and IoT, partnering with global technology vendors and serving enterprise customers.
