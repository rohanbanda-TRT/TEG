## Overview

Technoville Consultants Pvt. Ltd. is an Ahmedabad-based IT services company offering digital marketing, video animation, social media marketing, content writing, email marketing, website development, application development, POS software, business process outsourcing, and accounting/taxation services. A related entity, Technoville Consultants Private Limited, was incorporated in Ahmedabad on August 27, 2004.

## Company Details

- **Location:** C-808, Stratum @ Venus Grounds, Opposite Jhansi ki Rani Statue, Nehrunagar, Manekbag, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380015
- **Team size:** ~24 employees (as of May 2025, per LinkedIn); one profile lists 201–500 (likely reflects a different/related entity size record)
- **Directors:** Siddharth Sanjay Shah and Nirav Rajul Shah
- **Founded:** Company profiles show both a 2004 registration and a 2020 "founded" date reported on LinkedIn — not reconciled in this pass
- **Services:** Digital marketing, video animation, social media marketing, content writing, email marketing, website development, app development, POS software development, BPO, taxation & accounting

## TEG Participation

Confirmed TEG 2024 exhibitor. Technoville director **Nirav Shah** was featured in the "Tech Talks at Gujarat Tech Expo 2024" podcast series (Episode 36: "Nirav Shah, Technoville Consultants"), a promotional/interview series run in connection with the event, per his own LinkedIn post referencing the appearance and directly confirmed via the episode itself (Chai Time with ManthanBaba). Note: this is a different Nirav Shah than the one at Eternal Web (Ep 11 of the same series) and Technotery's founder Vedang Shah — several TEG-exhibiting founders share common Gujarati names.
