## Overview
Dev IT Simplified is the public-facing brand of **Dev Information Technology Ltd (DEV IT)**, an Ahmedabad-headquartered IT services provider specializing in digital transformation. The company organizes its services around what it calls the "ABCD" framework — AI Consulting & Development, Blockchain & Web3, Cybersecurity, and Datacenters — alongside cloud modernization, enterprise applications, application development, and managed IT services. It is a substantially larger and longer-established company than most other TEG 2024 exhibitors researched in this batch.

## Company Details
- Founded 1997 — over 28 years in operation.
- Headquartered at 14, Aaryans Corporate Park, Thaltej-Shilaj Rd, Thaltej, Ahmedabad – 380059.
- Team size: approximately 1,500 employees ("technologists, solution architects, and software engineers").
- Client base: 4,000+ clients worldwide across 11+ industries (including BFSI, healthcare, manufacturing, government), with a stated 90% client retention rate.
- Specialties: cloud services (AWS, Azure, hybrid), enterprise applications (Microsoft Dynamics 365, Power Platform), AI and automation, cybersecurity and managed IT operations, custom software/mobile development, blockchain/Web3.
