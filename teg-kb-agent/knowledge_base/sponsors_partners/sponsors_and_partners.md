### Why sponsor (organizer's pitch)
- Exclusive domain authority — sponsorship slots are limited and non-repetitive; once a brand locks a category, direct competitors are excluded from it
- Cross-industry exposure across manufacturing, healthcare, retail, real estate, agriculture
- Omnichannel visibility — venue signage, digital promotions, print PR, regional media
- Elite C-suite networking alongside keynote speakers and industry leaders
- Reach to 15,000+ visitors (SMEs/MSMEs/business owners)
- Association with the "AI revolution" / regional digital transformation narrative
