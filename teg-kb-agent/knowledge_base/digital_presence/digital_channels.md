## Mobile app (re-verified August 27, 2026 — re-fetched directly, not taken from prior summary)
TEG operates a dedicated event-networking mobile app, **"Tech Expo Gujarat"** (package `com.greenapex.techexpogujarat`), listed on:
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.greenapex.techexpogujarat
- Apple App Store: https://apps.apple.com/in/app/tech-expo-gujarat/id6504392774
- Indus Appstore: https://www.indusappstore.com/apps/business/tech-expo-gujarat/com.greenapex.techexpogujarat

**Verification note:** the Google Play and Apple App Store pages themselves could not be independently fetched/rendered (both returned no extractable content on repeated attempts — likely JS-heavy listing pages). The app's existence and publisher were instead confirmed directly via the **Indus Appstore** mirror, which lists:
- **App name:** Tech Expo Gujarat
- **Publisher:** **Green Apex Technolabs LLP** (4.0★, 17 MB, "Verified" badge)
- **Description:** framed as "a pioneering two-day event taking place in Ahmedabad, Gujarat," expecting 3,000+ attendees — i.e., stale/2024-era copy, not updated for TEG 2026's 3-day/15,000+ scale
- **Permissions requested:** storage, internet, phone-call management, camera

**Entity-name note:** Nilay Khandhar's organizer profile (`organizers_team/nilay_khandhar.md`) names his company **"Green Apex Solutions"** (and separately, "Green Apex Solutions Limited" as a past 2014–2022 role). The Indus Appstore listing names the app's publisher as **"Green Apex Technolabs LLP."** These may be the same "Green Apex" brand under a different or newer corporate entity, or a distinct related entity — this was not further reconciled, so treat the Khandhar/app-publisher link as likely but not fully confirmed identical.

This is the same "TEG App" referenced generically in `exhibitors/exhibitors_directory.md` ("networking via the TEG App") and `sponsors_partners/sponsors_and_partners.md` ("in-app one-to-one messaging") — the QR-code/social-feed/exhibitor-brochure feature claims from the previous pass could not be independently re-verified this round (source pages unfetchable) and have been removed pending re-confirmation; only what was directly re-verified via Indus Appstore is retained above.
