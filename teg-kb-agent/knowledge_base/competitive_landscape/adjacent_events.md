## FITAG National Tech Expo 2026

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Organizer | **Federation of IT Associations of Gujarat (FITAG)** — an association of 45+ IT associations, distinct from AIMED |
| Dates | **9–10 January 2026** (already occurred as of this KB pass) |
| Venue | Helipad Exhibition Center, Hall No. 2, **Gandhinagar** |
| Scale claims | 150+ exhibitors, 10,000+ channel partners/visitors, 250+ national delegates, 7,080 sq. m. exhibition area |
| Focus | Broader "IT ecosystem" framing — AI/ML seminars, technical workshops, CSR and e-waste awareness activities, "Make in India" innovator showcase (50+) |
| AIMED / TEG mention | **None found** in any source checked |

This is the closest direct competitor found: same state, similar IT/tech-ecosystem framing, a Gandhinagar venue roughly 25km from TEG's GUCEC (Ahmedabad), and a "national" (not just Gujarat) delegate framing that slightly out-reaches TEG's own positioning. It ran in January 2026, well before TEG 2026's November dates, so the two aren't calendar-competing for the same week — but they likely compete for some of the same exhibitor budget and sponsor attention within the same fiscal year.

## What this means for TEG 2026 positioning (analysis, not sourced)

Of the three, **FITAG is the most direct comparator** — same state, same "IT ecosystem" framing, and a scale claim (10,000+ channel partners) in the same order of magnitude as TEG's own 15,000+ visitor target, even though FITAG's own number is a "channel partners" figure and TEG's is a "visitors" figure (not directly comparable without knowing FITAG's definition). EFY and TECH VAPI both skew toward electronics/industrial manufacturing rather than TEG's IT-services/AI/SaaS-heavy exhibitor base, so they're closer to "adjacent calendar noise" than a head-to-head competitor for the same sponsor dollars.
