# FAQ — TEG 2026

> **Document type:** Official on-site FAQ
> **Event:** Tech Expo Gujarat 2026 (TEG 2026)
> **Source:** techexpogujarat.com FAQ section
> **Last updated:** August 2026

---

### What is Tech Expo Gujarat 2026?
A 3-day event bringing together technology companies, AI solution providers, SMEs & MSMEs decision-makers, investors, startups and industry leaders under the theme "Beacon of Rising Innovation & AI," aiming to position Gujarat as India's premier technology and innovation hub.

### Will there be networking opportunities?
Yes — structured networking sessions and informal meetups are part of the event.

### What is the scale of TEG 2026?
Expected to be the largest edition yet: 15,000+ visitors, 250+ exhibitors, 25+ keynote speakers, 7,000+ sq. meters of exhibition floor — nearly double TEG 2024's scale (8,000 visitors, 125+ exhibitors).

### When and where is TEG 2026 taking place?
27–29 November 2026 (3 days) at GUCEC (GMDC Ground), Ahmedabad, Gujarat — 7,000+ sq. meters of exhibition space.

### Is TEG 2026 only for IT companies?
No. It's designed for the wider business ecosystem: IT/tech companies exhibit; SMEs & MSMEs come to discover AI/tech solutions; entrepreneurs come for emerging-tech education, networking, and keynotes; investors/VCs scout startups and technologies.

### Why should I exhibit at TEG 2026?
Access to 15,000+ business visitors, multi-industry exposure, high-quality leads, and strong networking — all on one platform.

### What industries will be present?
Manufacturing, retail, logistics, healthcare, finance, education, and more — plus (per the homepage industry icons) automobile, power & energy, agriculture, electronics, pharmaceutical, jewelers, and textile.

### What types of booth options are available?
Multiple booth sizes and premium locations depending on business needs and branding preferences (3x3m, 3x6m, 6x6m, 3x9m corner, and a startup-oriented Catalyst Zone). Early exhibitors get priority booth selection, better visibility, and special pricing benefits. Full details in `exhibitors/exhibitors_directory.md`.

### How many exhibitors are expected?
**250+** exhibitors/innovators (organizer target for TEG 2026).

Business owners, founders, CEOs, IT leaders, technology professionals, investors, startups, students, consultants, government representatives, and technology enthusiasts can be relevant attendees.

*(Source: techexpogujarat.com/industries/, re-verified August 27, 2026)*

The official industries page (techexpogujarat.com/industries/) lists 18 industries: FMCG, Travel, Insurance, Manufacturing, Healthcare, Retailers, Finance institutions, Government Entities, Jewellers, Educational Institute, Textile/Garments, Electronics, Pharma, Startups, Ceramic, Agriculture, Automobiles, Real Estate.

*(Source: techexpogujarat.com/industries/, directly re-verified August 27, 2026)*

Yes. Startups are explicitly listed as one of the 18 target industries/ecosystem segments. Startup founders may participate as visitors, exhibitors, pitch applicants, or ecosystem partners.

*(Source: techexpogujarat.com/industries/)*

### Are the 2026 dates and venue confirmed?
**Yes.** TEG 2026 is confirmed for **27–29 November 2026** at **GUCEC (Gujarat University Convention and Exhibition Centre), Ahmedabad** — this has been independently re-verified multiple times, including via third-party exhibitor pages (e.g. Perigeon's own event page states the same dates/venue). What is *not* yet published is the detailed session agenda, exact ticket pricing, and the full exhibitor/speaker roster — see `event_overview/event_info.md` and `INDEX.md`'s "Known Data Gaps" table for what's actually still open.

*(Source: techexpogujarat.com, events.techexpogujarat.com, re-verified via third-party exhibitor pages August 27, 2026)*
