| **Catalyst Zone (startup)** | 2m × 2m | 2 | — | — | **₹35,000\*** |

### Infrastructure included with every open stall
- Modular stall structure (white Octanorm panels)
- Fascia with company name
- Carpet flooring
- Spotlights: 3 (3×3) · 6 (3×6) · 12 (6×6) · 9 (3×9)
- Power sockets (15 Amp): 1 (3×3) · 2 (3×6) · 3 (6×6) · 3 (3×9)
- Furniture:
  - 3×3: 1 table + 2 chairs
  - 3×6: 2 tables + 4 chairs
  - 6×6: 2 tables + 2 round glass tables + 2 sofas + 6 chairs
  - 3×9: 2 tables + 1 round glass table + 1 sofa + 4 chairs

### Included with every stall package (all sizes)
- TEG Community Network Portal access
- 1:1 curated business meetings (pre-scheduled)
- One-to-one in-app messaging to visitors
- Visibility: logo on website + on-ground visibility

### Catalyst Zone (2m × 2m) — startup package details
