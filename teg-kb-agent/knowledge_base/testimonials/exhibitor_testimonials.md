## ✅ Attributed Testimonials (TEG 2024 — from official sponsors brochure)

Source: `teg-sponsors-brochure.pdf`, "The TEG Impact (Year 2024)" page. These are TEG-published, on-the-record quotes with named attribution and photos. **Safe to quote in outreach and decks.** These are audience/speaker/partner voices, not exhibitor-company voices — do not attribute them to an "exhibitor" persona.

### Sonu Sharma
**Role:** Inspirational Speaker and Leadership Coach
**Context:** TEG 2024 keynote speaker
**Quote:**
> "I used to think tech talent was mostly in Bangalore, but after coming here, I realized Gujarat has an amazing force of tech people! The start itself is so powerful that I can only imagine how big the future results will be."

### Savjibhai Dholakia
**Role:** Founder & Chairman, HK Export Pvt. Ltd.
**Context:** TEG 2024 keynote speaker (diamond-industry entrepreneur, widely known for the "Hare Krishna Exports" bonus story)
**Quote:**
> "This Tech Expo is important not just for Gujarat, but for all of India. You have provided a space where people can learn how to use technology with emotion in business. I am glad to see this happening at such a large scale here."

### Chitrak Shah
**Role:** Founder & MD, Shivalik Group; Chairman, CREDAI Ahmedabad
**Context:** TEG 2024 keynote speaker
**Quote:**
> "I initially thought it would be a small event, but seeing the magnitude, I realized the scale. It is an amazing experience to see 5,000 to 6,000 people together at a technology expo here. I have never seen this kind of scale in technology in Gujarat before."

### Kanaksinh Rana
**Role:** Director, GTPL (TEG 2024 Title Sponsor)
**Context:** TEG 2024 keynote speaker / title-sponsor representative
**Quote:**
> "First time you've shown that there are so many tech companies in Gujarat that are delivering advanced tech solutions to international companies. This was not known before to many. Today, businesses want native tech solutions and are getting it. What you've done here has determined that it is Gujarat's time to lead the future of tech."
