# Aniruddh Nagodra — Co-Founder & CEO, FactoHR

> **Company:** factoHR (Version Systems Pvt Ltd)
> **Role:** Co-Founder & Chief Executive Officer
> **Location:** Rajkot, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Aniruddh Nagodra is the Co-Founder and CEO of **factoHR**, an HR automation and talent-management platform headquartered in Rajkot, Gujarat. He has over 15 years of experience spanning human resources and business development, and previously co-founded Version Systems Pvt Ltd, a post-sales support company.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- 15+ years of experience in HR and business development.
- Previously served as Co-Founder & CEO of Version Systems Pvt, a company providing post-sales support services.
- Holds a Bachelor of Engineering from Saurashtra University (2008–2012).
- Is a certified NLP (Neuro-Linguistic Programming) practitioner.
- Co-founded factoHR, launched in April 2016.

---

## 3. Domain Expertise / Company Detail

factoHR is an HR automation platform (payroll, HRMS, talent management) that, per company-sourced figures, serves roughly 2.6 million employees across 3,500+ organizations, positioning it as one of India's larger HR-tech platforms operating out of Gujarat.
