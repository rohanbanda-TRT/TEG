## 1. Executive Bio

Satyarth Srivastava is the Honorary Chairman Elect and Director of GESIA IT Association (Gujarat's IT industry association). He also serves as Director and COO of Adit Microsys, an IT solutions company. He plays a key leadership role in Gujarat's IT ecosystem through his positions at GESIA and his operational leadership at Adit Microsys.
