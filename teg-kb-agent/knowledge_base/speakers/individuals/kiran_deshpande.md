# Kiran Deshpande — Former CEO, Tech Mahindra; Co-Founder, Mojo Networks

> **Company:** Mojo Networks (acquired by Arista Networks); formerly Tech Mahindra
> **Role:** Former CEO, Tech Mahindra; Co-Founder & President, Mojo Networks; TiE Global Board Member
> **Location:** India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Kiran Deshpande is a veteran technology executive with 40+ years of business experience. He served as the former CEO of Tech Mahindra and later co-founded Mojo Networks, where he served as President until its acquisition by Arista Networks in August 2018. He is a Trustee on the TiE Global Board of Trustees and has been actively involved in mentoring and guiding entrepreneurs through TiE's global network.

*(Source: related_events.md, line 72; techwikipadia.com/nefconnect/kiran-deshpande/)*
