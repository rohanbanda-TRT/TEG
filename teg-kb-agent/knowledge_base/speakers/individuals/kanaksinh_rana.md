# Kanaksinh Rana — Director, GTPL

> **Company:** GTPL Hathway Limited
> **Role:** Co-Founder & Director
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Kanaksinh Rana is a Co-Founder and Director of **GTPL Hathway Limited**, a major Indian cable TV distribution and broadband services company headquartered in Gujarat. He has been part of the founding team and is credited with playing a key role in the company's networking and market-building strategy in the state.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- One of the founding members of GTPL (Gujarat Telelink Pvt Ltd, later GTPL Hathway).
- Credited with insightful networking that helped GTPL secure a reported ~70% market share in Gujarat's cable distribution market.
- Also involved in the operations of Gujarat News and other regional/local television channels tied to the GTPL ecosystem.

---

## 3. Domain Expertise / Company Detail

GTPL Hathway is a cable TV distribution and broadband services company, providing digital cable television in around 169 towns across India, alongside broadband internet services. It is a listed company (NSE/BSE) and one of India's larger regional cable MSOs (multi-system operators).
