# Vishal Mehta — Chairman & MD, Infibeam Avenues Ltd

> **Company:** Infibeam Avenues Ltd; also Chairman & MD, Rediff.com
> **Role:** Founder, Executive Chairman & Managing Director
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Vishal Ajitbhai Mehta is the founder of **Infibeam Avenues Ltd**, a publicly listed Indian digital payments and enterprise software company, where he serves as Executive Chairman and Managing Director. Following Infibeam Avenues' acquisition of a majority stake in Rediff.com, Mehta also took on the role of Chairman & MD of Rediff.com while continuing his Infibeam Avenues leadership.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Holds a degree in Operations Research and Industrial Engineering from Cornell University, and a management degree from MIT Sloan School of Management.
- Spent roughly a decade in the United States before founding Infibeam, including a role at Amazon's headquarters focused on technology revitalization and corporate development.
- Founded Infibeam in 2007 alongside a cohort of Amazon colleagues, initially as an e-commerce platform, later evolving into Infibeam Avenues, a digital payments and enterprise software company.
- Under his leadership, the company is reported to process over $20 billion in transactions annually, serving more than a million merchants.
- Following Infibeam Avenues' acquisition of a majority stake in Rediff.com, appointed Chairman & MD of Rediff.com while remaining Chairman & MD of Infibeam Avenues.
