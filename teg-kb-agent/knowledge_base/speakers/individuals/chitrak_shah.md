# Chitrak Shah — Founder & MD, Shivalik Group

> **Company:** Shivalik Group
> **Role:** Founder & Managing Director; Chairman, CREDAI Ahmedabad
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Chitrak Shah is the Founder and Managing Director of **Shivalik Group**, an Ahmedabad-based real estate development group, and Chairman of **CREDAI Ahmedabad** (Confederation of Real Estate Developers' Associations of India, Ahmedabad chapter), the city's primary real-estate industry body.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Began his entrepreneurial journey in 1998, co-founding a company named "S.N. Developers" with his father.
- The "Shivalik" brand itself was launched in 2001, growing into the present-day Shivalik Group real estate business.
- Served as Vice President of CREDAI Ahmedabad from August 2017 to October 2023.
- Has served as Chairman of the Board of CREDAI Ahmedabad since October 2023.

---

## 3. Domain Expertise

Real estate development in Ahmedabad/Gujarat, and real-estate industry advocacy/policy through his CREDAI Ahmedabad chairmanship — CREDAI is the apex body representing private real estate developers across India.
