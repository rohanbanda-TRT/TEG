## 1. Executive Bio

Sharad Bansal is the Founder and Managing Partner of Warmup Ventures, an early-stage venture capital firm focused on "amplifying tenacious founders." He is also a Charter Member of TiE Bangalore and an IIT Delhi alumnus (Batch of 2015, B.Tech). He has been featured as a Tycoon (Investor) on India's premier startup show "Bharat ke Super Angels."
