# Dharmesh Acharya — Co-Founder, ZeroThreat

> **Company:** ZeroThreat (ZeroThreat.ai)
> **Role:** Co-Founder
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Dharmesh Acharya is the Co-Founder of ZeroThreat, an AI-powered web application and API security (pentesting) platform. ZeroThreat combines agentic AI pentesting with a high-performance scanning engine, positioning itself as delivering deeply validated vulnerability detection up to 10x faster than conventional tools, and reports serving 5,000+ organizations.
