## 1. Executive Bio

Mihir Joshi is the Managing Director of GVFL Limited, one of India's most respected early-stage venture capital firms. He has over 25 years of venture capital investment experience across diverse sectors and has shaped GVFL into a multi-stage, multi-sector venture investor. Based in Ahmedabad, he has been instrumental in funding and mentoring more than 150 startups throughout his career.
