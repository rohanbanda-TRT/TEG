# Kishorsinh Zala — Chairman, Powertrac Group

> **Company:** Powertrac Group (incl. Powertrac Solar Projects Ltd)
> **Role:** Chairman
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Kishorsinh Zala is the Chairman of **Powertrac Group**, a Gujarat-based group active in solar energy manufacturing, EPC (engineering, procurement, construction), and independent power production (IPP). He also holds several community/industry leadership titles connected to Gujarat's Rajput and solar-business communities.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Holds a bachelor's degree in engineering.
- Chairman of Powertrac Group, which operates Powertrac Solar Projects Ltd and Powertrac Industries, among other group entities.
- Holds multiple concurrent community/industry leadership titles: National President of the Rajput Business Forum; State President of the Zalawad Business Forum; President of the Gujarat Solar Business Forum; Vice President of the Gujarat Solar Federation and of the National Solar Energy Federation of India (Gujarat state chapter).
- Based in Ahmedabad.

## 3. Domain Expertise / Company Detail

Powertrac Group operates in India's renewable energy sector — solar manufacturing, EPC, and IPP. The group has announced expansion into battery storage manufacturing (a 10 GWh BESS container manufacturing facility, per trade press), indicating a broader push into energy storage alongside solar.
