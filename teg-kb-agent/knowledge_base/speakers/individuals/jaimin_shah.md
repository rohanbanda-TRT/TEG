# Jaimin Shah — CEO & MD, Dev Information Technology

> **Company:** Dev Information Technology Limited (DEV IT); Dev IT North America Ltd
> **Role:** Chief Executive Officer & Managing Director
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Jaimin Shah is the CEO and Managing Director of **Dev Information Technology Limited (DEV IT)**, an NSE-listed IT consulting and services company headquartered in Ahmedabad, and its Canadian subsidiary Dev IT North America Ltd. He has 20+ years (one source cites 29+ years) of experience in the IT industry and is a well-known figure in Gujarat's IT and startup ecosystem through leadership roles in NASSCOM, ASSOCHAM, GCCI, TiE, and other industry bodies.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Holds a bachelor's degree in Computers.
- CEO & MD of Dev Information Technology Ltd (India) and Dev Info-Tech North America Ltd (Canada).
- Grew DEV IT toward becoming, in the company's own positioning, a "next generation global IT consulting and end-to-end IT services company," including a Canadian acquisition that formed the North America subsidiary — recognized by "Invest Toronto" as among the most successful companies in Canada (per company-sourced material).
- Elected Chairman of the Domestic Council of NASSCOM (India's apex IT trade association) — reported as the youngest and first Gujarati to chair a NASSCOM council.
- Serves as Co-Chairman of ASSOCHAM Gujarat and Vice Chairman of TiE Ahmedabad, among other industry-body roles (also cited: GESIA, GCCI, QCI, ICBC, AMA).
- Received "Outstanding IT Entrepreneur of the Year" from the Ahmedabad Management Association (AMA) and "Alumni of the Year" from Dharamsinh Desai (institute/university).

---

## 3. Domain Expertise / Company Detail

Dev Information Technology Limited (devitpl.com) is a publicly listed (NSE) IT company delivering digital solutions built on cloud, automation, and data technologies, serving enterprise clients in India and internationally through its North America subsidiary.
