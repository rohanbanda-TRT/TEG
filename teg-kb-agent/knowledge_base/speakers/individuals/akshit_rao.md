# Akshit Rao — International Payment Business Head, Razorpay

> **Company:** Razorpay
> **Role:** International Payments Business Head
> **Location:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Akshit Rao (professional profiles list his name as "Akshith Rao") leads the International Payments business at Razorpay, one of India's largest fintech/payments companies. In this role he oversees Razorpay's cross-border payments offering, which allows Indian businesses to accept and process payments in 130+ currencies via cards, wallets, and local bank transfers.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Prior experience noted in public profiles includes Tata Communications and Gokhelo before joining Razorpay.
- At Razorpay, active publicly (LinkedIn, X/Twitter) on topics of cross-border payments, exporters, and international payment infrastructure, including directly engaging with prospective business users about Razorpay's international card/bank-transfer support as an alternative to PayPal.
- Razorpay itself (founded 2014, HQ Karnataka) secured a Payment Aggregator–Cross Border licence from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), enabling regulated inward and outward cross-border transactions — the business area Rao's role sits within.

---

## 3. Domain Expertise

- Cross-border/international payments strategy, payment aggregation, fintech regulatory licensing (RBI Payment Aggregator–Cross Border framework), and support for Indian exporters/global-facing businesses.
