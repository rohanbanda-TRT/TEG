# Siddharthsinh Vaghela — Head of Business Development, GTPL

> **Company:** GTPL Broadband Pvt. Ltd.
> **Role:** Head of Business Development / Head of Enterprise Business
> **Location:** Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Siddharthsinh Vaghela holds a senior business-development leadership role at GTPL Broadband, one of Gujarat's largest cable TV/broadband/enterprise connectivity providers. Public sources describe him variously as "Head of Enterprise Business" and "Head of Business Development" at GTPL, with in-depth knowledge of the telecom and media sectors.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Senior leader within GTPL Broadband's enterprise business division.
- Associated with GTPL's expansion into enterprise digital infrastructure — for example, GTPL Broadband signed a Letter of Intent with Radian Arc (a Submer company) for a three-year collaboration to deploy GPU-as-a-Service (GPUaaS) and AI solutions across India, targeting universities, governments, and enterprises. Vaghela's specific involvement in that particular deal beyond his general enterprise-business leadership role is not independently confirmed from available sources.

## 3. Domain Expertise

- Telecom and media/broadband enterprise business development.
- GTPL's core business spans cable TV distribution, broadband, and (per recent partnership activity) enterprise/AI infrastructure services (GPUaaS).
