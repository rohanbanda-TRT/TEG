# Savjibhai Dholakia — Founder & Chairman, Hari Krishna Exports

> **Company:** Hari Krishna Exports Pvt Ltd (HK Group / HK Exports)
> **Role:** Founder & Chairman
> **Location:** Surat, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Savji Dhanji Dholakia (commonly Savjibhai Dholakia) is a prominent Indian businessman and the founder and chairman of **Hari Krishna Exports**, one of India's largest diamond manufacturing and exporting companies. He is a well-known figure in the Indian diamond and jewelry industry, recognized both for HK Group's commercial scale and for his philanthropic activity through the Dholakia Foundation. He was awarded the **Padma Shri** (India's fourth-highest civilian honor) in 2022.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Born 12 April 1962 in Dudhala village, Amreli district, Gujarat, into a Leva Patel family.
- Studied only until the 4th standard and dropped out of school at around age 13.
- At age 12, traveled over 500 km from his home village to Surat, India's diamond-trading hub, to find work, arriving with no money.
- Joined his paternal uncle's diamond business in Surat; his brothers Himmat and Tulsi later joined him.
- Co-founded the diamond business with his brothers in 1984; the company **Hari Krishna Exports** was formally established in 1992 by Savjibhai and his three brothers (Ghanshyam, Himmat, Tulsi Dholakia).
- Grew HK into a major diamond exporter — by 2014 the company had approximately 9,000 employees; it now exports to over 86 countries with a reported turnover around USD 1.5 billion.
- Launched the jewelry brand **KISNA** in 2005, which grew into one of India's largest diamond jewelry brands with over 6,250 retail outlets.
- Known for extravagant employee incentive programs — Diwali bonuses that have included houses, cars, mopeds, and in some cases luxury cars for employees.
- Founded the **Dholakia Foundation**, the group's philanthropic arm, focused on environmental conservation (including building 144 lakes/water bodies to date) and social welfare.
- Received the Padma Shri civilian honor in 2022.

## 3. Domain Expertise / Company Detail

Hari Krishna Exports (HK Group) is a diamond manufacturing and export company headquartered in Surat, Gujarat — a global hub for diamond cutting and polishing. The group's businesses span rough diamond sourcing, cutting/polishing, export, and consumer-facing jewelry retail (KISNA brand). Savjibhai is closely associated with employee-welfare-driven corporate culture and rural/environmental philanthropy in Gujarat.
