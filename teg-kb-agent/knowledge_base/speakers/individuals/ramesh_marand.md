## 1. Executive Bio

Ramesh Marand (also referenced as Ramesh Marand Ahir in some professional listings) is a technology executive who founded and led Tridhya Tech, a technology solutions provider, and currently serves as a Director at Shaligram Infotech, overseeing strategic direction and operations. He is also listed as associated with related ventures including Hexagon Innovations Pvt Ltd and Inexture Solutions LLP.
