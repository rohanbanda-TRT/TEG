# Sonu Sharma — Inspirational Speaker & Leadership Coach

> **Company:** Dynamic India Group (Dynamic India Infonet Pvt Ltd)
> **Role:** Founder, Motivational Speaker & Corporate Trainer
> **Location:** Faridabad, Haryana, India (operates pan-India)

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Sonu Sharma is an Indian motivational speaker, author, educator, and business consultant, widely described as one of India's most-watched speakers online with claimed views exceeding 1 billion across his digital content. He is the founder of **Dynamic India Group**, a training and consulting outfit focused on leadership, direct sales, and personality development. He speaks on motivation, leadership development, entrepreneurship, network marketing, financial growth, and personal transformation.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Born 11 November 1981 in Faridabad, Haryana, India.
- Completed a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) from DAV College, Chandigarh; was active in debate and public-speaking circuits during college.
- Has 20+ years of experience in leadership training and direct/network sales, and has built a large presence in the direct-selling and corporate training space in India.
- Founded Dynamic India Group, which trains individuals and organizations in leadership, sales, and personality development.
- Authored books including "Dare to Dream" and "Paise Kaise Banaye" (How to Make Money).
- Received a "Best Motivational Speaker Award" from the Indian Achievers Forum (cited as 2015 in secondary sources; not independently verified beyond aggregator bios).
- His YouTube channel content is reported to have reached over 800 million views across 114 countries.

## 3. Domain Expertise

- Motivational and leadership speaking, corporate training, and personality development.
- Network marketing / direct sales training — a core specialty of Dynamic India Group.
