# Ankur Warikoo — Entrepreneur, Investor, Content Creator & Author

> **Company:** WebVeda / India Genius Challenge / Monzy (founder); formerly nearbuy.com, Groupon India
> **Role:** Serial Entrepreneur, Angel Investor, Content Creator, Author
> **Location:** India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Ankur Warikoo is an Indian entrepreneur, angel investor, content creator, and bestselling author, born 25 August 1980. He is best known as the founding CEO of Groupon's India business and later as founder and CEO of nearbuy.com, and today as one of India's most-followed personal-finance/career/self-improvement content creators, with a combined community reported at 5 million+ across YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, X (Twitter), and Facebook (LinkedIn's own bio cites 16M+ community).

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Education: BS in Physics from Hindu College, Delhi University; MS in Physics from Michigan State University; MBA from the Indian School of Business (ISB), Class of 2006, where he received the Young Leader Award.
- **Groupon India (2011–2015):** Founding CEO of Groupon's India business (launched 2011); later Head of Groupon APAC (2013–2015).
- **nearbuy.com (2015–2019):** Founded and served as CEO of nearbuy.com, described as India's largest local-commerce/lifestyle marketplace at the time, which was later acquired by Paytm.
- **Content creation & education (2019–present):** Built a large personal-brand content business across YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, and other platforms, covering entrepreneurship, personal finance, career growth, and productivity; runs online courses through which 150,000+ students have reportedly enrolled.
- **Author:** Best known for the books "Do Epic Shit" (2021, reached #1 on India's Nielsen bestseller list) and its follow-up "Get Epic Shit Done"; described in his own bio as a 6X bestselling author.
- **Investing/mentorship:** Listed as a mentor for 100X.VC (an early-stage Indian VC/accelerator platform), reflecting an active angel-investing and startup-mentoring role.
- Founder of WebVeda and India Genius Challenge (per his LinkedIn bio); "Monzy" is also listed as a founder credit there, though further detail on this venture was not independently verified in this research pass.
