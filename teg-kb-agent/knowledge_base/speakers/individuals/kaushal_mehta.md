# Kaushal Mehta — Co-Founder, Motif

> **Company:** Motif (Motif India Infotech Pvt. Ltd.; acquired by TTEC in 2017)
> **Role:** Founder / Co-Founder; currently VP & GM, TTEC India
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Kaushal Mehta founded Motif, a digital customer-experience (CX) and BPO company, in 2000 in Ahmedabad, alongside his wife Parul Mehta. The pair left careers in the US in 1998 to build the company in India. Motif grew into a provider of essential fraud-prevention and community-moderation services for major online brands in travel, retail, and e-commerce, operating out of Ahmedabad and Manila. Motif was acquired by TTEC (a global customer-experience outsourcing company) in 2017; Kaushal Mehta currently serves as VP and GM for TTEC in India.
