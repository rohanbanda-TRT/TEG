# Dr. Gnanvatsal Swami — Life Coach & Eminent Speaker, BAPS

> **Company:** BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha (Bochasanwasi Shri Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha)
> **Role:** Life Coach, Motivational Speaker & Spiritual Leader
> **Location:** Affiliated with BAPS (global speaking circuit; India-based order)

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Dr. Gnanvatsal Swami (also written Gnanvatsaldas Swami) is a life coach, motivational speaker, and spiritual leader within **BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha**, a globally active Hindu spiritual and social organization. He is known for large-audience "life-enhancing" seminars combining spirituality, motivation, and stress-management guidance, delivered across India and internationally.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Graduated as a mechanical engineer from BVM College of Engineering, Gujarat, before pursuing a spiritual path.
- Undertook his spiritual journey under the guidance of His Holiness Pramukh Swami Maharaj, the formal leader of BAPS and sixth spiritual successor of Bhagwan Swaminarayan.
- Over three decades of speaking experience; reported to have delivered more than 15,500 life-enhancement talks and interacted with audiences in over 32 countries.
- Has spoken at institutions including IIM Ahmedabad ("Leadership Conversations: Leadership & the Limitlessness of the Human Spirit") and at large public events internationally (e.g., an audience of 1,000+ in Jackson, Mississippi, USA, for a talk titled "Think Different, Be Different, Succeed Different").
- Holds two honorary Doctor of Literature degrees and has received the "Suryaratna — The Saint of Modern India" award.
- Author of the book "Inspired: Daily Wisdom for Holistic Living" (published via Amazon.in / Penguin imprint listing).

---

## 3. Domain Expertise

Speaking topics associated with him include "Positivity in Adversity," "Stress Management," "The Secret of Success: Unity," "Reinvent Evolve Lead," and "My Family, Happy Family" — a blend of spiritual teaching, personal development, and leadership motivation delivered under the BAPS institutional umbrella.
