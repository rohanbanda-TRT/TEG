## 1. Executive Bio

Pratul Shroff is a serial entrepreneur, venture investor, and philanthropist with over three decades of leadership in technology entrepreneurship and social impact. He founded and served as CEO of eInfochips, a pioneering product and semiconductor engineering company, and currently leads the Dr. K R Shroff Foundation. He is recognized for his contributions to technology entrepreneurship and philanthropic initiatives.
