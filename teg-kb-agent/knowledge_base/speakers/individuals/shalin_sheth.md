# Shalin Sheth — Founder & MD, Advait Energy Transitions Ltd (AETL)

> **Company:** Advait Energy Transitions Limited (AETL) / Advait Infratech
> **Role:** Founder & Managing Director
> **Location:** Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Shalin Sheth is the Founder and Managing Director of Advait Energy Transitions Limited, a power-transmission and renewable-energy infrastructure company he founded in 2009. Under his leadership, Advait evolved from a specialized transmission-solutions provider into a broader energy infrastructure organization spanning grid technologies, renewable EPC (engineering, procurement, construction), and green-energy systems.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Studied at the Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Ahmedabad.
- Previously worked at Adani Power Limited as a General Manager before founding his own venture.
- Founded Advait Energy Transitions Limited in 2009; the company built capabilities in optical ground wire, aluminium-clad steel wires, stringing tools, and emergency restoration systems for power transmission networks, later expanding into solar EPC, battery energy storage systems (BESS), and green hydrogen infrastructure.
- In January 2023, founded Advait Greenergy Private Limited (AGPL) as a dedicated vehicle to advance clean-energy transition projects.
- Profiled multiple times in 2026 by trade publications (Power Line, Renewable Watch, Indian Infrastructure, Electrical Mirror) as a leader in India's energy-transition sector.

---

## 3. Domain Expertise

- Power transmission infrastructure, renewable energy EPC, battery storage (BESS), green hydrogen, and grid technology — spanning both traditional power infrastructure and India's clean-energy transition.
