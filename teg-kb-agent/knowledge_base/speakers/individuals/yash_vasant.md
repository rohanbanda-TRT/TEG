# Yash Vasant — Managing Director, Vasant Group

> **Company:** Vasant Group
> **Role:** Managing Director; also Founder & National Director, CorporateConnections India/Nepal/Sri Lanka; Executive Director, BNI Gujarat
> **Location:** Ahmedabad / Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Yash Vasant is the Managing Director of the **Vasant Group**, a diversified group with holdings in education, trucking, warehousing, and global logistics. He is also a prominent figure in India's business-networking industry, holding franchise leadership for **BNI (Business Network International)** in Gujarat and founding **CorporateConnections** operations across India, Nepal, and Sri Lanka.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Managing Director of Vasant Group, with investments/holdings across Education, Trucking, Warehousing, and Global Logistics.
- Founder and Executive Director of BNI in Gujarat, India, where he built one of the country's largest non-government business-networking bodies — reported at over 4,000 members having generated over ₹6,000 crore (~$1 billion) in inter-member business revenue.
- Founder and National Director of CorporateConnections in India, Nepal, and Sri Lanka — a premier business network for larger business owners (minimum net worth threshold cited at $10 million), reported to have grown over 80% year-on-year under his leadership.
- Recognized as an "Enterprise Icon in India" by The Economic Times.

---

## 3. Domain Expertise

- Business networking and referral-based business development (BNI, CorporateConnections) at scale across Gujarat and South Asia.
- Diversified group management spanning logistics, warehousing, trucking, and education sectors via Vasant Group.
