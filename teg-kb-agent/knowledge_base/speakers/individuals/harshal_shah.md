# Harshal Shah — CEO, Elsner Technologies

> **Company:** Elsner Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
> **Role:** Founder & CEO
> **Cross-reference note**

---

Harshal Shah is the Founder & CEO of Elsner Technologies Pvt. Ltd., an Ahmedabad-headquartered IT solutions company (founded/led by him since 2006) specializing in web, mobile, eCommerce, and digital marketing/digital-transformation services for global clients.

He was a named speaker at **TEG 2024**, listed with affiliation "CEO – Elsner Technologies," and was a panelist on the Saturday, 21 December 2024 "Gold Sponsors" panel discussion titled **"The Future of Tech Solutions: Trends and Predictions Across Key Digital Sectors,"** moderated by Nachiket Patel (Digicorp), alongside Dharmesh Acharya (ZeroThreat), Aniruddh Nagodra (FactoHR), and Akshit Rao (Razorpay).

He is also a core organizer of Tech Expo Gujarat — for his full profile see `../../organizers_team/harshal_shah.md`.
