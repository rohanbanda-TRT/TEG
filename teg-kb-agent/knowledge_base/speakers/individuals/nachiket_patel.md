# Nachiket Patel — Co-Founder & VP Operations, Digicorp

> **Company:** Digicorp
> **Role:** Co-Founder & VP Operations
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Nachiket Patel is a Co-Founder and VP of Operations at **Digicorp**, an Ahmedabad-based software development and technical consulting company that helps startups and companies build software products. He has a hands-on background across the software development lifecycle and is active in Ahmedabad's startup and tech-community ecosystem.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Co-founded Digicorp alongside Abhishek Desai, Kuntal Shah, and Sanket Patel.
- Background spans roles as Developer, Tech Lead, and Project Manager across the software development lifecycle.
- Instrumental in developing Digicorp products including eClinc, an EHR (electronic health records) product for outpatient clinics.
- Serves as a board member at GESIA IT Association (Gujarat's IT industry association).
- Involved with or backing several Gujarat-origin startups/products: CricHeroes (an app for local cricketers), Petpooja, VastraApp, and Propeller.
- Also writes on Medium under his own byline.

---

## 3. Domain Expertise / Company Detail

Digicorp is a software product development and technical consulting company based in Ahmedabad, working with startups and enterprises to build software products; the company has also served as an internal incubator of sorts, with Digicorp-linked founders spinning out ventures like CricHeroes.
