## Executive Bio

Jainesh Shah is the Founder & CEO of **iCreative Technologies** (founded 2014, Ahmedabad, with co-founder Kalpesh Maheshwari) and Co-Founder & CTO of **Aktiv Software**, an offshore software outsourcing company. He has 15–18+ years of technology and business experience and manages 150+ software engineers across his two companies, with e-commerce and ERP expertise. Prior to Aktiv, he was Manager of Operations (India) at broadcasting company Sandhira.
