## Organizing entity
**AIMED TECH EXPO GUJARAT LLP** — described on-site as "a movement to position Gujarat at the forefront of India's and the world's technological future," run by a coalition of Ahmedabad-area IT company founders/CEOs.

**AIMED, decoded (added September 9, 2026):** **AIMED = Ahmedabad IT Management Executive Delegation**, a closed peer community for Ahmedabad IT company founders/CEOs. Per its own site: membership is **by reference and approval** (not open sign-up), events are closed-room sessions ("curated speakers, senior-only audience"), and attendance is limited to members and verified guests. It runs its own regular programming independent of TEG (monthly meetups, workshops, even a cricket league) — TEG is one AIMED initiative among several, not AIMED's sole purpose. (Source: aimedit.org — direct fetch returned HTTP 403; the above is reconstructed from aimedit.org's own indexed/cached text plus multiple AIMED-tagged LinkedIn posts from members, Sept 9, 2026.)
