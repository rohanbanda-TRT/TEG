# Harshal Shah — Founder & CEO, Elsner Technologies Pvt Ltd

> **Company:** Elsner Technologies Pvt Ltd (Elsner Group)
> **Role:** Founder & CEO
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Harshal Shah is the Founder and CEO of Elsner Technologies, a globally recognized IT solutions company he founded in 2006, specializing in web, mobile, eCommerce, and digital marketing services. With 20+ years of industry experience, he has grown Elsner from a startup into a multi-venture group and is a serial entrepreneur, mentor to young founders, and a regular speaker at industry events.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Founded Elsner Technologies in 2006 and has led it continuously since, building it into what the company's own site calls a "globally recognized" digital partner.
- Over 20 years of experience in technology and business; has founded three companies in total.
- Has led teams of 40+ members at Elsner.
- Beyond Elsner, he leads a portfolio of associated ventures: **Braincube**, **Link Publishers**, **EcomVA**, **MyAppGuru**, and the **ELDI institute** — spanning digital transformation, education/training, and e-commerce virtual-assistant services.
- Known for a leadership style described (on Elsner's own team page) as "radical transparency" and an "obsession with craftsmanship."
- Active mentor to young entrepreneurs and a regular speaker at industry conferences and events (e.g., listed as a speaker contact on BrightonSEO's speaker directory and TechWell's community).
