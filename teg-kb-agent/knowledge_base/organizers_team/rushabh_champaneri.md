## Executive Bio

Rushabh Champaneri is the CEO / Founder & Managing Director of **IOTTIVE Private Limited**, a complete IoT solutions company (mobile, embedded, cloud, and hardware/firmware development) based in Ahmedabad and founded in 2016. TEG's page credits him with "15+ years of experience in M&A, fundraising, and strategic advisory," helping businesses scale through innovation and growth strategy. He previously founded/led Bluepixel Technologies and worked as a Senior iOS/mobile developer. He has also been a featured speaker at IoT-focused industry events (e.g., IOTSHOW.IN).
