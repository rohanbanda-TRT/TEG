## Executive Bio

Saumil Patel is the Co-Founder & Director of **Xpro.ai**, an Ahmedabad-built event-technology platform for ticketing, exhibitions, and conferences. Xpro.ai is the event-tech platform that powers TEG's own ticketing infrastructure, making Patel's role a direct vendor-plus-organizer relationship. He shapes Xpro.ai's product vision and strategic partnerships. His prior experience includes roles at Green Apex Solutions/Green Apex Technolabs (fellow organizer Nilay Khandhar's company) and eTechMavens. He holds a Master's in Computer Science (IT) from GLS University (2015–2017).
