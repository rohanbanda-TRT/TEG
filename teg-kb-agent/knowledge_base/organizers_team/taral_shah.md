# Taral Shah — Director, Techify Solutions Pvt Ltd

> **Company:** Techify Solutions Pvt Ltd (also Co-Founder, Lomoso Solutions)
> **Role:** Director / Co-Founder
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Taral Shah is a Director and Co-Founder of Techify Solutions Pvt Ltd, an Ahmedabad-headquartered IT services and product-engineering company, and a Co-Founder of Lomoso Solutions, a local-business digitization platform. Per Tech Expo Gujarat's own "Meet Our Organizers" page, he is described as an AWS-certified systems architect with 15+ years of IT experience who has worked as "an influential technical leader for Cloud and Scalable technologies" with major multinational corporations, and who currently serves as CTO/VP Engineering for various Indian and US startups.

## 3. Company & Domain Detail

**Techify Solutions Pvt Ltd**
- Founded 2016; headquartered in Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, with a secondary US office in Sheridan, Wyoming.
- Positions itself as an "IT Consulting Partner" focused on product engineering, software development, DevOps automation, CRM/ERP integration, and AI-powered web and mobile app development, serving clients in education, healthcare, automobile, retail, logistics, and finance.
- ~51–200 employees; AWS Consulting and Networking Partner.
- Recognition: Grand Challenge winner at Vibrant Gujarat Summit 2018; "Trend Setter" award from the Gujarat Innovation Society.
- Notable in-house products: AI CardVault (business card scanning software), CloudLaunchPad (DevOps software).

**Lomoso Solutions Pvt Ltd**
- Co-founded by a group of five, aims to build a low-cost, integrated technology platform for customer engagement, branding, and digitization aimed at local/small businesses.
