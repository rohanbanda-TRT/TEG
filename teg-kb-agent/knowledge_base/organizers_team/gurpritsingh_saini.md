# Gurpritsingh Saini — CEO, AppAspect

> **Company:** AppAspect Technologies Pvt Ltd
> **Role:** Founder, Director & CEO
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

## 1. Executive Bio

Gurpritsingh Saini (public profiles also spell it "Gurupritsingh Saini") is the CEO, Founder, and Director of AppAspect Technologies Pvt Ltd, a mobile app and web development company headquartered in Ahmedabad, India, operating since 2011. Tech Expo Gujarat's own organizers page describes him simply as a "Tech Entrepreneur with 15 years of experience in the IT Industry."

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Holds a degree in Computer Engineering from Sardar Patel University (2004–2008).
- Founded AppAspect Technologies on 17 February 2011 and has led it as CEO/Founder/Director since.
- 15 years of experience in the IT industry per TEG's own bio.
- Technical background/skill set includes C++, C, Objective-C, SQLite, and iPhone/iOS development, reflecting his roots in early mobile-app engineering before scaling the company.
- Limited additional public information is available on career history prior to founding AppAspect — no earlier employers were identified in this research.

## 3. Company & Domain Detail

**AppAspect Technologies Pvt Ltd**
- Mobile app development and IT software solutions company, founded 2011; headquartered in Ahmedabad, with client-facing presence also referenced in the UK, USA, and Australia.
- Core services: native iOS (Swift) and Android (Kotlin) app development, Flutter, React Native, Laravel, WordPress/WooCommerce, and AI-powered app frameworks.
- Scale: ~36 employees, reported annual revenue around $3 million; has delivered 150+ iOS apps among a broad app portfolio spanning shopping, gaming, education, lifestyle, B2B e-commerce, and on-demand travel apps.
- Client base described as spanning startups, enterprises, and entrepreneurs worldwide since 2011.
