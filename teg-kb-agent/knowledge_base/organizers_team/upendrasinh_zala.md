## Executive Bio

Upendrasinh Zala is the Founder & CEO of **Neuramonks**, an enterprise AI and deep-tech consulting firm (a brand under Kshatrainfotech Pvt Ltd) headquartered in Ahmedabad, specializing in AI/ML consultancy, RAG systems, LLM development, and MLOps support. He has over a decade of IT experience. His prior ventures reportedly include Hawkium (digital marketing) and roles as Managing Director at Kshatrainfotech and in international business development at Winspire Web Solution. He holds an MCA from Gujarat Law Society's computer technology institute. He spoke on AI in pharma at a Gujarat Chamber of Commerce and Industry (GCCI) session in November 2025.
