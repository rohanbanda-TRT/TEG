## Executive Bio

Pruthviraj Zala is the Founder & Director of **AONE SEO Service Pvt Ltd**, an Ahmedabad-based digital marketing agency incorporated in 2013. He has 15+ years of experience in digital marketing strategy and execution. AONE SEO is also the agency that designed and promotes the Tech Expo Gujarat website itself, making Zala's involvement doubly practical: he is both a co-organizer and TEG's website vendor. He holds a B.Tech in Computer Engineering from Charotar University of Science and Technology (2010).
