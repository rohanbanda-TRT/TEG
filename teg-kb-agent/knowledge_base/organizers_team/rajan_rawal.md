## 1. Executive Bio

Rajan Rawal is the Founder and CEO of ThinkTanker, an Ahmedabad-based web and mobile application development company. Tech Expo Gujarat's own team page notes his B.E. in Computer Science and a stated preference for startup environments, with experience spanning e-commerce, custom applications, retail, HR, and mobile apps.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Holds a B.E. in Computer Science from Sarvajanik College of Engineering & Technology (2007–2010).
- Co-founded ThinkTanker alongside Nency Rawal, who serves as Business Development Manager & Co-Founder.
- Built ThinkTanker into a web/app development firm of roughly 62 employees serving clients across 11+ countries (US, UK, Singapore, Kenya, South Africa, Germany, Canada, Australia, Netherlands, Norway, UAE, Finland).
- Currently positions ThinkTanker's work around AI-driven personalization systems for e-commerce and retail.

---

## 3. Company & Domain Expertise

**ThinkTanker** is a web and mobile app development company based in Vastral, Ahmedabad, specializing in e-commerce development, custom web application development, and mobile app development (Android and iOS). Technology stack includes PHP, NodeJS, AngularJS, Laravel, Shopify, React Native, and ReactJS. The company's more recent positioning emphasizes AI-driven personalization for modern e-commerce.
