## Executive Bio

Divyesh Gohil is the Co-Founder of **The One Technologies**, an Ahmedabad-based web, mobile, and custom software development company established in 2010/2011. He has 12–14+ years of corporate and entrepreneurial experience, leads brand management and marketing, and was previously CEO of E-Static Infotech Pvt Ltd, a web development company he also founded. He holds a B.E. in Computer Engineering from L.D. College of Engineering.
