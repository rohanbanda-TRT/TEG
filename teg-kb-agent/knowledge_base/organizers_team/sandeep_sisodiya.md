# Sandeep Sisodiya — CEO, AppsRow Solutions

## 1. Executive Bio

Sandeepsingh Sisodiya (also styled "Sandeep Sisodiya") is the Co-Founder and CEO of AppsRow Solutions, a Webflow Premium Partner agency based in Ahmedabad and GIFT City, Gujarat, focused on building websites for B2B, SaaS, and AI companies. Tech Expo Gujarat's organizers page describes him as having "Transformed 150+ IT Agency CEOs with SALES GROWTH STRATEGIES," functioning as an IT Sales Coach focused on organizational development.

## 2. Background & Career History

- Over a decade of experience in the global IT industry.
- Has mentored/coached IT agency CEOs on sales growth strategy — TEG's own bio cites "150+ IT Agency CEOs"; a separate profile (eChai Ventures speaker page) cites "250+ IT CEOs" mentored — the exact figure varies by source, but both confirm a substantial track record as an IT sales/growth coach.
- Has spoken at IIM Indore and at AMA (Ahmedabad Management Association), per his eChai Ventures speaker bio.
- Specializes in B2B growth strategy, IT sales coaching, and digital transformation via the Webflow platform, positioning himself at the intersection of business development and web strategy — helping founders and marketing leaders turn websites into predictable revenue systems.
- Built AppsRow Solutions into what is described as "one of India's most recognized Webflow agencies," partnering with 25+ global B2B brands and delivering 300+ Webflow projects across India, Southeast Asia, North America, and Europe.

## 3. Company & Domain Detail

**AppsRow Solutions**
- Webflow Premium Partner agency based in Ahmedabad and GIFT City, Gujarat; 300+ projects delivered since 2018.
- Core services: Webflow development and design, website migrations (from WordPress, Shopify, HubSpot, etc.), CMS integrations, custom workflows, SEO/AEO (AI Engine Optimization), conversion rate optimization, and "agentic Webflow" development (integrating AI/Claude-driven automation into Webflow sites).

- Recognition: Webflow Premium Partner status, 4.7/5 rating on Clutch, Client-First Certified Expert; clients include Y Combinator- and Zoom Ventures-backed companies.
