# Tejas Shah — Founder, MagnusMinds IT Solutions

> **Company:** MagnusMinds IT Solution
> **Role:** Founder
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Tejas Shah is the Founder of MagnusMinds IT Solution, an Ahmedabad-based IT outsourcing and software development company specializing in .NET, Power BI, and data engineering/analytics consulting. Per Tech Expo Gujarat's own "Meet Our Organizers" page, he has "over 20 years building successful businesses" and is a Microsoft Certified Professional who emphasizes collaboration and continuous learning. He is one of the core coalition of Ahmedabad IT-company founders who organize Tech Expo Gujarat.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- Holds a Master of Science in Computer Application and Information Technology from Gujarat University.
- Career background prior to founding MagnusMinds includes roles as Software Engineer at Arihant Software, Module Leader at Hidden Brains Private Limited, and Project Manager at Excellence Infonet Solution Private Limited (per The Org / RocketReach aggregator profiles — not independently verified beyond these secondary sources).
- Previously Co-Founder and Director at TechnoBrains IT Solution Private Limited before founding MagnusMinds (per RocketReach; TechnoBrains' current CEO on the TEG organizing team is Bhavik Shah, a separate person).
- Founded MagnusMinds IT Solution, which has operated in Ahmedabad for 15+ years.

---

## 3. Company & Domain Expertise

**MagnusMinds IT Solution** is an Ahmedabad-based IT outsourcing company offering dedicated .NET, ASP.NET Core, MVC, Angular, React, Power BI, Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, and AI developers. Core technology stack includes C#, Entity Framework, LINQ, NodeJS, MS SQL, SSIS/SSRS, PowerApps, Docker, and data pipeline tools (ELK, Kafka, AWS SQS, Azure EventGrid). The company reports 200+ completed international projects across 12+ countries and 51–200 employees. MagnusMinds has itself exhibited at Tech Expo Gujarat 2024 (the company maintains a dedicated "Tech Expo Gujarat 2024" page on its own website).

## 4. TEG Involvement

Tejas Shah is listed among the core "Meet Our Organizers" on Tech Expo Gujarat's official team page, alongside eleven other Ahmedabad-area IT founders/CEOs (Taral Shah, Harshal Shah, Bhavik Shah, Sandeep Sisodiya, Gurpritsingh Saini, Pinakin Soni, Nirav Shah, Nilay Khandhar, Tapan Patel, Rajan Rawal, Vishal Rajpurohit). This coalition collectively runs AIMED TECH EXPO GUJARAT LLP, positioned as "a movement to position Gujarat at the forefront of India's and the world's technological future." MagnusMinds' own participation as a TEG 2024 exhibitor reinforces the direct, practical tie between his company and the event he helps organize.
