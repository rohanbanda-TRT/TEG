## Executive Bio

Mehul Shah is the CEO & MD of **Elision Group** (Elision Technologies Pvt Ltd / Elision TechnoLab LLP), an Ahmedabad-based unified telecommunications and VoIP solutions provider he has led for over a decade (CEO/Founder of Elision TechnoLab since 2014; CEO/MD of Elision Technologies since 2019). His earlier career includes Chief Product Officer at OnlineVoIP Solutions and VP of Development at Adunik Telecommunications. He is an alumnus of Nirma University, Ahmedabad. TEG's page frames his vision as building "earth's most customer-centric company" for unified communications.
