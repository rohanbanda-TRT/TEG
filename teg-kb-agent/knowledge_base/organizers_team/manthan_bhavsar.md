## Executive Bio

Manthan Bhavsar is the Founder & CMO of **Metizsoft Solutions Pvt Ltd**, an Ahmedabad-based web/mobile/e-commerce and IT services company. He has 12+ years of experience in e-commerce, fintech, and platform engineering. He previously held business-development roles at Sigma Solve and Samvith Technologies. He is also an entrepreneur/investor and hosts the podcast "Chai Time with ManthanBaba," on which fellow organizers (e.g., Nilay Khandhar, Saumil Patel) have appeared as guests.
