# Vishal Rajpurohit — Co-Founder & CTO, ViitorCloud

> **Company:** ViitorCloud Technologies
> **Role:** Co-Founder & CTO
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India (ViitorCloud also has offices in Zürich, Washington D.C., and Port Louis)

---

## 1. Executive Bio

Vishal Rajpurohit is Co-Founder and CTO of ViitorCloud, an AI-focused software and platforms engineering firm founded in 2010. He is also the organizer of Laracon India — Asia's only edition of the Laravel community's flagship conference, running since 2020 — and is well known in the global Laravel/PHP community as a conference organizer, speaker, and emcee.

---

## 2. Background & Career History

- 15+ years building software, products, and technology solutions.
- Began his career in 2005 building an inventory system in .NET, later moving into security research and open-source web development.
- Co-founded ViitorCloud in 2010; the company has since expanded to four offices worldwide (Ahmedabad HQ, Zürich, Washington D.C., Port Louis).
- Launched LaraCopilot in 2025, an AI-powered Laravel application builder.
- Community leadership: Organizer of Laracon India since 2020 (1,200+ attendees in 2023, reportedly the largest Laravel event of its kind in India); Emcee at Frontend Nation; Board Advisor for Certification for Laravel; member of the Swiss Laravel Association; Advisory Council member at Open Source Day; listed as "Mentor" on Tech Expo Gujarat's team page. Has hosted 8+ international conferences, attended 50+, delivered 20+ talks, and traveled to 35+ countries.

## 3. Company & Domain Expertise

**ViitorCloud Technologies** is an AI-first software and platform engineering company (founded 2010) specializing in scalable architecture, product development, and AI-driven solutions. ViitorCloud has showcased AI innovation at global events including Laracon US 2025. Rajpurohit describes his engineering philosophy as "start with the problem, not the technology" — practical solutions built to survive production environments.

**Laracon India**, which he organizes, is Asia's sole regional edition of Laracon and India's leading Laravel community event/conference.
