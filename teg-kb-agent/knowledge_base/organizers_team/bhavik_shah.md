# Bhavik Shah — CEO, TechnoBrains Business Solutions LLP

> **Company:** TechnoBrains Business Solutions LLP
> **Role:** Founder & CEO
> **Location:** Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India

## 1. Executive Bio

Bhavik Shah is the Founder and CEO of TechnoBrains Business Solutions, an Ahmedabad-founded, globally operating software development company established in 2008/2009. Tech Expo Gujarat's own organizers page credits him with "over 15 years of experience driving innovation in IT" and describes his commitment to "transformative technology, strategic leadership, and fostering growth in competitive markets."

## 2. Background & Career History

- 15+ years of experience in the IT industry, spanning work with startups and MNCs before founding and running his own company.
- Holds a Masters degree from Northumbria University and a Bachelor's degree (specializing in Information Technology) from KS Business Management School.
- Nearly 15 years of experience specifically in technology development, primarily web and mobile application development, prior to and alongside his time at TechnoBrains.
- 500+ LinkedIn connections; active on the platform under multiple listed profiles (a primary profile identifies him as "TechnoBrains Business Solutions"; some public records also list him as Founder/Director of an associated "MindTech Consultancy").

## 3. Company & Domain Detail

**TechnoBrains Business Solutions LLP**
- Software development company established c. 2008–2009; offices in Ahmedabad, India and San Francisco, California, USA.
