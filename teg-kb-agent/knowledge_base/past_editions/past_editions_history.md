## TEG 2024 — Gujarat's first & largest IT tech expo (the original)
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Dates | 20–21 December 2024 (2 days) |
| Venue | Vigyan Bhavan, Science City, Ahmedabad |
| Venue size | 3,000+ sq. meters |
| Attendees | 8,000+ |
| Exhibitors | 125+ |
| Speakers | 20+ named (see `speakers/speakers_and_agenda.md`) |
| Sponsors | 50+ |

Framed as Gujarat's first large-scale IT/tech expo, blending exhibition (app dev, software, ERP, CRM, digital marketing companies), knowledge sessions/conferences, and networking. Included a dedicated "Experience Zone" with AI and hologram tech demos. Full named exhibitor and sponsor lists are in `exhibitors/exhibitors_directory.md` and `sponsors_partners/sponsors_and_partners.md`.

### Notable attendee/speaker testimonials (from TEG 2024/2026 promo material)
- **Chitrak Shah** (Founder & MD, Shivalik Group; Chairman, CREDAI Ahmedabad): described being struck by the scale — "5,000 to 6,000 people together at a technology expo," unlike anything he'd seen in Gujarat tech before.
- **Savjibhai Dholakia** (Founder, HK Exports Pvt Ltd): called it important not just for Gujarat but for India, providing a platform for youngsters to overcome fear of new technology.
- **Sonu Sharma** (Inspirational speaker & leadership coach): noted he used to think tech talent was concentrated in Bangalore, and was surprised by Gujarat's tech workforce.
- **Vatsal Shah** (Business Catalyst & CEO Coach; VP, Magento Association): framed the expo as proof Ahmedabad doesn't need to look to Dubai or Singapore for a high-level IT expo.
- **Ramesh Marand** (Director, Tridhya Tech): compared the organizing effort to running an IPO or a grand wedding; suggested future editions should have every tech company invite their own clients.
- **Yash Vasant** (Vasant Group; Founder, BNI Ahmedabad): praised Gujarat's growing tech ecosystem and government policy support.
- **Shalin Sheth** (Founder & MD, Advait Energy Transitions Ltd): noted Gujarat's historical entrepreneurial strength hadn't always translated to large-scale IT corporates, and framed TEG as a turning point toward competing globally, not just against Bangalore/Pune.

## TEG Business Retreat 2025 — the "sharpening" chapter
An invite-only, high-impact 2-day offsite for ~150+ IT founders, CEOs, product leaders, and investors — a deliberate shift from expo-floor scale to boardroom depth. Full detail (speakers, VCs, sponsors, agenda, pricing) is in `related_events/related_events.md`. Headline result cited by organizers: ₹1.5 crore in funding raised in one day.

## TEG Ignite 2026 — curtain-raiser for TEG 2026
A single-evening AI & growth session (3 April 2026) that doubled as the official launch event for TEG 2026. Full detail in `related_events/related_events.md`.
