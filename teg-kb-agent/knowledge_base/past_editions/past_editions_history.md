## Data Quality Note

**TEG 2024 figures used throughout this KB (8,000+ attendees, 125+ exhibitors, 20+ named speakers, 50+ sponsors) come from techexpogujarat.com's own event pages and have been cross-checked across multiple files (`INDEX.md`, `event_overview/event_info.md`, `sponsors_partners/sponsors_and_partners.md`) without contradiction.**

A different figure of "10,000+ attendees" for TEG 2024 was proposed for inclusion here but could not be verified against any source in this KB or independently re-fetched — it directly conflicts with the 8,000+ figure that IS sourced and repeatedly cross-checked, so it was not added. Similarly, a claimed "40,000+ community members" figure for TEG 2024 has no traceable source and was not added. If either number turns up on an official page in a future crawl, it should be reconciled explicitly against the existing 8,000+ figure rather than silently replacing it.

### The "3,000+" vs. "8,000+"/"15,000+" attendee-figure split (added September 9, 2026)

Two independent third-party promo pages — Green Apex's TEG 2024 page and TechnoBrains' TEG 2026 page — both state "3,000+ attendees," conflicting with TEG's own 8,000+ (2024 actual) / 15,000+ (2026 target) figures used throughout this KB. A broader search of independent exhibitor and press pages (5 more checked beyond those two, both 2024- and 2026-dated) finds the same split repeats:

| Source | Edition referenced | Figure used | Page date/framing | Independent of AIMED? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Green Apex (green-apex.com) | TEG 2024 | **3,000+** | Exhibitor promo page | Yes — AIMED member (per own site) |
| TechnoBrains (technobrains.io/techexpo-gujarat-2026/) | TEG 2026 | **3,000+** | Exhibitor promo page | Yes — AIMED member (per own site) |
| digitalterminal.in (press) | TEG 2024 | **3,000+** | Published Dec 12, 2024 — explicitly pre-event/future-tense ("will bring together more than 3,000 participants") | Yes — independent press |
| The One Technologies (theonetechnologies.com) | TEG 2024 | **3,000+** ("over 3,000 attendees") | Exhibitor promo page | Yes |
| Codevision Technologies (thecodevision.com) | TEG 2024 | **3,000+** ("over 3,000 participants anticipated") | Exhibitor promo page | Yes — explicit AIMED member (see `organizers_team/organizers_and_team.md`) |
| Fintegration (fintegrationfs.com) | TEG 2026 | **15,000+** (matches official) | Exhibitor promo page — "more than 15,000 visitors, 250 exhibitors and 25 speakers" | Yes |

**Count: 5 of 8 independently-checked third-party pages use "3,000+"; 3 use the official "15,000+."** This is a real majority for "3,000+" by page count — but it is **not** presented here as reason to override the sourced 8,000+/15,000+ figures, for two reasons the KB should keep in mind:

1. Every "3,000+" instance found either explicitly describes TEG **2024** (where the digitalterminal.in press article shows "3,000+" was the *pre-event* expectation, published Dec 12, 2024 — 8 days before the event) or is TechnoBrains' 2026 page apparently **reusing the same 2024-era "3,000+" copy** rather than an updated 2026 figure. No source claims to be reporting a *post-event actual* attendance different from 8,000+.
2. TEG's own site, independently re-fetched September 9, 2026 (not carried forward from a prior pass), still states 8,000+ as the 2024 actual, and frames 2026's 15,000+ target explicitly as "nearly double" that 8,000+ base — i.e., the organizer's own current copy is internally consistent with 8,000+, not 3,000+.

**Working theory, not a resolution:** "3,000+" most likely originated as TEG 2024's *pre-event* footfall estimate, which several exhibitors' promo pages (including, oddly, one 2026 page) never updated after the event reportedly exceeded it. This is a plausible explanation, not a confirmed one — no source explicitly states "our earlier 3,000+ estimate was revised to 8,000+." **The KB's primary figures remain 8,000+ (2024 actual) / 15,000+ (2026 target)**, per the existing sourcing above; this table exists so the conflict is visible rather than silently smoothed over, per this KB's Zero-Confusion policy (see `SKILL.md`).

## TEG 2024 — Gujarat's first & largest IT tech expo (the original)
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Dates | 20–21 December 2024 (2 days) |
| Venue | Vigyan Bhavan, Science City, Ahmedabad |
| Venue size | 3,000+ sq. meters |
| Attendees | 8,000+ |
| Exhibitors | 125+ |
| Speakers | 20+ named (see `speakers/speakers_and_agenda.md`) |
| Sponsors | 50+ |

Framed as Gujarat's first large-scale IT/tech expo, blending exhibition (app dev, software, ERP, CRM, digital marketing companies), knowledge sessions/conferences, and networking. Included a dedicated "Experience Zone" with AI and hologram tech demos. Full named exhibitor and sponsor lists are in `exhibitors/exhibitors_directory.md` and `sponsors_partners/sponsors_and_partners.md`.

### Notable attendee/speaker testimonials (from TEG 2024/2026 promo material)
- **Chitrak Shah** (Founder & MD, Shivalik Group; Chairman, CREDAI Ahmedabad): described being struck by the scale — "5,000 to 6,000 people together at a technology expo," unlike anything he'd seen in Gujarat tech before.
- **Savjibhai Dholakia** (Founder, HK Exports Pvt Ltd): called it important not just for Gujarat but for India, providing a platform for youngsters to overcome fear of new technology.
- **Sonu Sharma** (Inspirational speaker & leadership coach): noted he used to think tech talent was concentrated in Bangalore, and was surprised by Gujarat's tech workforce.
- **Vatsal Shah** (Business Catalyst & CEO Coach; VP, Magento Association): framed the expo as proof Ahmedabad doesn't need to look to Dubai or Singapore for a high-level IT expo.
- **Ramesh Marand** (Director, Tridhya Tech): compared the organizing effort to running an IPO or a grand wedding; suggested future editions should have every tech company invite their own clients.
- **Yash Vasant** (Vasant Group; Founder, BNI Ahmedabad): praised Gujarat's growing tech ecosystem and government policy support.
- **Shalin Sheth** (Founder & MD, Advait Energy Transitions Ltd): noted Gujarat's historical entrepreneurial strength hadn't always translated to large-scale IT corporates, and framed TEG as a turning point toward competing globally, not just against Bangalore/Pune.
