## For an IT/Tech Service Company (ERP, Cloud, App Dev)

### Access to Decision-Makers Across Industries

TEG 2026 provides direct access to **15,000+ business visitors (SMEs, MSMEs, decision-makers)** from diverse industries including **manufacturing, automobile, power & energy, agriculture, educational institute, healthcare, electronics, pharmaceutical, jewelers, textile — plus retail, logistics, and finance** (Source: event_info.md, lines 29, 49; exhibitors_directory.md, line 46).

This is not an IT-only event — it's explicitly designed for Gujarat's broader business ecosystem to discover and adopt AI/tech solutions, making it ideal for ERP, cloud, and app development companies seeking B2B clients across sectors (Source: event_info.md, line 23).

### Pre-Scheduled B2B Meetings and Networking

All stall packages include **pre-scheduled 1:1 curated business meetings** and **in-app one-to-one messaging with visitors**, enabling structured lead generation rather than relying on casual footfall (Source: exhibitors_directory.md, lines 43, 48).

Exhibitors also get networking via the TEG App with **founders/CEOs/industry leaders** (Source: exhibitors_directory.md, line 49).

### Multi-Industry Exposure

The event offers **multi-industry exposure (manufacturing, retail, logistics, healthcare, finance, etc.)** (Source: exhibitors_directory.md, line 50), allowing IT service companies to showcase solutions to potential clients from multiple sectors in one location.

### Live Product Demonstration Opportunities

Exhibitors have **live product demonstration opportunities** (Source: exhibitors_directory.md, line 51), which is particularly valuable for ERP, cloud, and app development companies that need to show complex systems in action.

### Brand Positioning

Exhibitors benefit from **AI & future-tech brand positioning** (Source: exhibitors_directory.md, line 47), positioning their services at the forefront of technological innovation.

## For an AI/Deep-Tech Startup

### Dedicated Startup-Focused Zone: Catalyst Zone

TEG 2026 offers a **Catalyst Zone (2m x 2m, startup-focused)** stall package specifically designed for startups, priced more affordably than standard stalls (Source: exhibitors_directory.md, line 41).

### Experience Zone for AI Demos

TEG 2024 featured a dedicated **"Experience Zone" with AI and hologram technology demos — interactive AI installations and holographic visuals, branded as "Experience the Future"** (Source: exhibitors_directory.md, line 148). This demonstrates TEG's commitment to showcasing cutting-edge AI technology, which is likely to continue in 2026.

### Access to Investors and VC Network

TEG has a proven track record of facilitating startup funding. The **TEG Business Retreat 2025 reportedly helped facilitate ₹1.5 crore in funding raised in one day** (Source: event_info.md, line 45).

The Retreat 2025 attracted **VCs including Ekatva, Unicorn India Ventures, Enrission India Capital, GVFL Limited, Morphosis, Growth Capital, Vyoma, BM Capital, Warmup Ventures, Blinc VC, IIMA Ventures, Ashika VC, Caret Capital, Seafund, DevX** (Source: sponsors_partners.md, line 70). This network is likely to be present or accessible at TEG 2026 as well.

### Mission Aligned with Startup Ecosystem

TEG's mission is **"To empower industries to lead the AI revolution by connecting a collaborative ecosystem of investors and innovators to drive global technology transformation and leadership"** (Source: event_info.md, line 40), which directly aligns with AI/deep-tech startup needs for investor connections and market validation.

### Networking with Industry Leaders

Startups benefit from **networking via the TEG App with founders/CEOs/industry leaders** (Source: exhibitors_directory.md, line 49), providing access to potential partners, customers, and mentors.

### Affordable Entry Point

The Catalyst Zone offers a **2m x 2m startup-focused stall** with basic infrastructure (1 table, 2 chairs, 2 spotlights, 1x 15A power socket) at a more accessible price point than larger stalls (Source: exhibitors_directory.md, line 41).

## For a Non-Tech Company (Potential Sponsor)

### Brand Exclusivity in Your Category

Sponsorship offers **exclusive domain authority — sponsorship slots are limited and non-repetitive; once a brand locks a category, direct competitors are excluded from it** (Source: sponsors_partners.md, line 13). This ensures no direct competitors in your sponsorship tier.

### Elite C-Suite Networking

Sponsors get **elite C-suite networking alongside keynote speakers and industry leaders** (Source: sponsors_partners.md, line 16), providing high-value networking opportunities with decision-makers.

### Omnichannel Visibility

Sponsorship provides **omnichannel visibility — venue signage, digital promotions, print PR, regional media** (Source: sponsors_partners.md, line 15), ensuring comprehensive brand exposure across multiple channels.

### Cross-Industry Exposure

Sponsors benefit from **cross-industry exposure across manufacturing, healthcare, retail, real estate, agriculture** (Source: sponsors_partners.md, line 14), allowing non-tech companies to reach diverse business sectors.

### Access to 15,000+ Business Visitors

Sponsors have **reach to 15,000+ visitors (SMEs/MSMEs/business owners)** (Source: sponsors_partners.md, line 17), providing massive audience access.

### Association with AI Revolution Narrative

Sponsors gain **association with the "AI revolution" / regional digital transformation narrative** (Source: sponsors_partners.md, line 18), positioning their brand alongside technological innovation.

### Multiple Sponsorship Tiers Available

TEG 2026 offers **multiple sponsorship tiers** including Title Sponsor (6m x 6m stall with largest logo on all branding, entry-gate branding, media/PR coverage, 5 VIP passes, 20 visitor passes, full attendee data), Official AI Partner, Official Real Estate Partner, Official Automobile Partner, Official Banking Partner, and many more category-specific partnerships (Source: sponsors_partners.md, lines 21-38).

### VIP Passes and Data Access

Higher-tier sponsors receive **VIP passes and full attendee data** (Source: sponsors_partners.md, line 23), enabling targeted follow-up and relationship building.

### Stage Presence and Recognition

Many sponsorship tiers include **stage mention, on-stage trophy, website logo, social media announcement** (Source: sponsors_partners.md, lines 24-33), providing public recognition and brand visibility.

## Lead Qualification Questions

When a prospect inquires about TEG 2026, use these 10 questions to qualify their intent:

1. Are you interested in visiting, exhibiting, sponsoring, or speaking?
2. What industry does your company operate in?
3. What solution or technology are you looking for or showcasing?
4. Are you a startup, SME, enterprise, investor, or service provider?
5. Do you want a booth, sponsorship package, speaking slot, or product pitch?
6. How many representatives will attend from your organization?
7. Do you require a private meeting or product demonstration?
8. What is your expected participation budget?
9. Do you need accommodation or transport arrangements?
10. When should the event team contact you?

TEG 2026 offers tailored value propositions for different company types:

- **Non-Tech Companies (Sponsors):** Brand exclusivity, C-suite networking, omnichannel visibility, and association with AI revolution narrative
