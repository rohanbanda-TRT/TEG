## What it is
TEG 2026 is a 3-day AI and technology exposition in Ahmedabad that brings together technology companies, AI solution providers, SMEs & MSMEs, investors, startups and industry leaders under one roof. It is positioned as Gujarat's biggest tech expo, aiming to establish Gujarat as India's premier technology and innovation hub. It's explicitly not IT-only — it's designed for the state's broader business ecosystem (manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture, retail, finance, education, etc.) to discover and adopt AI/tech solutions.

> For TEG's stated goals, the problem it was created to solve, the mechanism by which it delivers value, and a per-persona pain-point library, see `event_goals_and_problem.md`.

## Vision, Mission, Values
- **Vision:** To position Gujarat as a global innovation hub by creating an influential platform where industry leaders and entrepreneurs collaborate to shape the future of intelligent enterprises.
- **Mission:** To empower industries to lead the AI revolution by connecting a collaborative ecosystem of investors and innovators to drive global technology transformation and leadership.
- **Values:** Build with integrity, collaborate without boundaries, innovate with purpose — championing Gujarat's technological growth at every step.

## The TEG Journey (organizational narrative)
- **Chapter 1 — The Spark (2024):** Concept, formation, launch. A group of 12 core CXO members conceived the idea (reportedly during a conversation in Goa) and built Gujarat's first and largest tech expo — TEG 2024.
- **Chapter 2 — The Sharpening (2025):** Focus, positioning, elevation. TEG moved from the expo floor to the boardroom with an invite-only, CXO-first business retreat (TEG Business Retreat 2025), which reportedly helped facilitate ₹1.5 crore in funding raised in one day.
- **Chapter 3 — The Leap (2026):** Opportunity, scale, movement. TEG 2026 is described as the biggest leap yet — a 3-day mega expo to unite Gujarat's tech forces on the global innovation map.

## Industries represented
